## Anthropic Engineering: How we contain Claude across products

*Anthropic Engineering*

**Anthropic details its security strategy for agentic AI across Claude.ai, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork, emphasizing that containment at the environment layer is more reliable than model-level supervision.**

Anthropic 详细介绍了其在 Claude.ai、Claude Code 和 Claude Cowork 中的 agent AI 安全策略，强调环境层的隔离比模型层的监督更可靠。

The article outlines three risk categories—user misuse, model misbehavior, and external attackers—and three corresponding defense layers: the environment (sandboxes, VMs), the model (prompts, classifiers), and external content (tool permissions). A key insight is that probabilistic model defenses will never be 100% effective, so deterministic boundaries like sandboxes are essential. The authors share real-world vulnerabilities they discovered, such as project hooks executing before user trust prompts, users acting as injection vectors via phishing, and data exfiltration through approved domains like `api.anthropic.com`. They conclude that the "blast radius" of an agent must be capped at the environment level, using tools like gVisor and hypervisors, rather than relying solely on model alignment.

文章概述了三种风险类别——用户滥用、模型行为不当和外部攻击者——以及三个相应的防御层：环境（沙箱、VM）、模型（提示词、分类器）和外部内容（工具权限）。一个关键见解是，概率性的模型防御永远无法达到 100% 有效，因此像沙箱这样的确定性边界至关重要。作者分享了他们发现的现实世界漏洞，例如项目 hooks 在用户信任提示之前执行、用户通过网络钓鱼成为注入向量，以及通过 `api.anthropic.com` 等批准域名进行的数据泄露。他们的结论是，必须在环境层限制 agent 的“爆炸半径”，使用 gVisor 和虚拟机监控程序等工具，而不是仅仅依赖模型对齐。

## No Priors — Building an Autonomous Delivery Experience with DoorDash Co-Founders Andy Fang and Stanley Tang

*No Priors*

**DoorDash's robotics strategy proves that starting from the customer use case—rather than the technology—allowed them to identify a gap in the market that neither sidewalk robots nor robotaxis could fill.**

DoorDash 的机器人战略证明，从客户用例（而非技术）出发，使他们能够发现一个市场空白，这是人行道机器人和机器人出租车都无法填补的。

Andy Fang and Stanley Tang explain that DoorDash has been a robotics company since 2018, but they initially tried partnering with existing startups. They realized that sidewalk robots were too slow (2-3 mph) for their average 3-5 mile delivery, while robotaxis were overkill (4,000 lbs) for carrying food. This led them to build "DOT" in-house—a 300lb vehicle that travels 20mph and navigates both roads and bike lanes. A critical advantage is their data: with 10 billion deliveries, they know exactly where to drop off food (solving the "last 100 feet" problem) in ways Google Maps doesn't. They also discuss "Ask DoorDash," an AI feature that has increased grocery basket sizes by 40% and caused 50% of restaurant orders to be from new places, proving that conversational interfaces unlock latent demand. As Stanley Tang notes, "The next DoorDash is not gonna be someone that builds the exact same version of DoorDash... It's gonna be something like, okay. Do we incorporate AI, agent to commerce, how to incorporate autonomy, robotics?"

Andy Fang 和 Stanley Tang 解释说，DoorDash 自 2018 年以来就是一家机器人公司，但他们最初尝试与现有的初创公司合作。他们意识到，对于他们平均 3-5 英里的配送距离来说，人行道机器人太慢了（2-3 英里/小时），而机器人出租车对于运送食物来说又大材小用（4000 磅）。这促使他们内部构建了“DOT”——一辆重 300 磅、时速 20 英里、既能在道路上也能在自行车道上行驶的车辆。一个关键优势是他们的数据：通过 100 亿次配送，他们确切知道在哪里送餐（解决了“最后 100 英尺”的问题），这是 Google Maps 做不到的。他们还讨论了“Ask DoorDash”，这是一项 AI 功能，使杂货购物篮大小增加了 40%，并导致 50% 的餐厅订单来自新地方，证明了对话式界面释放了潜在需求。正如 Stanley Tang 所说，“下一个 DoorDash 不会是有人构建完全相同版本的 DoorDash……它将是某种东西，比如，我们是否要整合 AI，agent 到商业，如何整合自主性、机器人技术？”
