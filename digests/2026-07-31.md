## AI & I by Every — Best of the Pod: Wired's Kevin Kelly on Why AI Is a 50-year Overnight Success

*AI & I by Every*

**The Takeaway: AI is likely a "compound" made of many cognitive elements we haven't identified yet, not a single elemental force, which means we will eventually engineer thousands of different types of intelligence.**

Wired co-founder Kevin Kelly draws a fascinating parallel between the current confusion over AI and the 18th-century misunderstanding of electricity, where even Isaac Newton had totally wrong theories about "effluvium." Just as electricity turned out to be a compound phenomenon, Kelly suspects intelligence is also a complex compound of different cognitive elements—like salt is made of sodium and chlorine—that we haven't yet deconstructed. He argues that humans are just one "edge species" in a high-dimensional space of possible minds, and we will soon fill that space with many engineered intelligences that don't look like us at all.

Kelly also shares a counterintuitive take on the generative AI boom: he believes 99.999% of the 50 million images generated daily are created for an "audience of one," where the joy lies in the co-creation process, not the final product. He tested this by using AI to generate ten novels about a fictional city founded by Leonardo da Vinci and Martin Luther, purely for his own amusement. "The joy of creating it was better than reading it," he notes, suggesting that AI will become a form of active imagination and entertainment rather than just a production tool. His advice for navigating this rapidly changing frontier: "Don't aim to be the best, aim to be the only."

Wired 联合创始人 Kevin Kelly 将当前对 AI 的困惑与 18 世纪对电的误解进行了有趣的类比，当时即使是艾萨克·牛顿也对“流质”有着完全错误的看法。正如电最终被证明是一种复合现象一样，Kelly 怀疑智能也是由我们尚未解构的不同认知元素组成的复杂化合物——就像盐是由钠和氯组成的一样。他认为人类只是高维可能思维空间中的一个“边缘物种”，我们很快就会用许多不像我们的工程智能来填充这个空间。

Kelly 还分享了一个关于生成式 AI 繁荣的反直觉观点：他认为每天生成的 5000 万张图像中，有 99.999% 是为“唯一的观众”创造的，乐趣在于共同创造的过程，而不是最终产品。他通过使用 AI 生成十部关于由达芬奇和马丁·路德建立的虚构城市的小说来验证这一点，纯粹为了自娱。“创造它的乐趣比阅读它更好，”他指出，这表明 AI 将成为一种积极的想象力和娱乐形式，而不仅仅是生产工具。他关于应对这个快速变化的前沿的建议是：“不要试图做最好的，要试图做唯一的。”
