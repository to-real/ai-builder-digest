## 🔥 今日主题：Evals & Assessment
**AI adoption is shifting from "does the model work?" to "does the system work for the specific workflow?"**

**Aaron Levie:** AI diffusion is rate-limited by evals, not just model capability. General model benchmarks are helpful, but enterprises need specific workflow evals to automate confidently. You cannot automate what you cannot assess.

**Madhu Guru:** Hill climbing on evals means picking a dimension that matters and optimizing for it. Whether improving quality, lowering cost, or fixing tool-calling failures, use your failure mode taxonomy as a compass. Iterate with better harnesses and model selection until you move the needle.

## AI & I by Every — Microsoft’s Vision for an Internet Made for Agents With CTO Kevin Scott (Best of the Pod)

*AI & I by Every*

**The future of AI isn't just smarter models, but an "agentic web" where agents can take action via open protocols like MCP.** Scott argues that while model reasoning is ahead of product usage ("capability overhang"), the real bottleneck is infrastructure for memory and action. He draws a parallel to the early web: just as HTTP connected pages, MCP connects agents to tools and data. He also defends the "craft" of coding against automation, noting that woodworkers once debated power tools too—tools change, but the choice of how to use them remains.

AI 的未来不仅仅是更聪明的模型，而是一个 "Agentic Web"，Agent 可以通过 MCP 等开放协议执行操作。Scott 认为，虽然模型的推理能力已经超过了产品的使用场景（即 "capability overhang"），但真正的瓶颈在于记忆和行动的基础设施。他将这与早期的网络进行了类比：就像 HTTP 连接了网页一样，MCP 连接了 Agent 与工具及数据。他还维护了编程的 "工艺" 不受自动化的冲击，指出木匠曾经也争论过电动工具的问题——工具在变，但如何使用它们的选择权依然存在。
