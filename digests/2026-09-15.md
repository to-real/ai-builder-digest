## No Priors — Redefining Chip Architecture with Arm CEO Rene Haas

*No Priors*

**The Takeaway:** The CPU is the unsung hero of AI, orchestrating tokens while GPUs generate them, and AI is now revolutionizing how we design chips.

**核心观点：CPU 是 AI 的无名英雄，在 GPU 生成 token 的同时负责协调，而 AI 正在彻底改变我们设计芯片的方式。

Rene Haas, CEO of ARM, argues that while GPUs get the attention for training, CPUs are critical for the orchestration and "token trucking" required in inference systems. He reveals that 80-90% of ARM engineers use AI daily for verification and validation, cutting down the most time-consuming part of chip design. Haas predicts that within five years, we could go from idea to a GDSII file (manufacturing ready) in under a year for simpler designs, though physical design tools are still maturing. He also identifies the supply chain—specifically data center construction, wafer capacity, and memory allocation—as the primary bottleneck for AI growth, not demand.

ARM CEO Rene Haas 认为，虽然 GPU 因训练而备受关注，但 CPU 对于推理系统所需的协调和 "token 运输" 至关重要。他透露，ARM 80-90% 的工程师每天都在使用 AI 进行验证和确认，这缩短了芯片设计中最耗时的部分。Haas 预测，在五年内，对于较简单的设计，我们可以从构思到 GDSII 文件（制造就绪）在一年内完成，尽管物理设计工具仍在成熟中。他还指出，供应链——特别是数据中心建设、晶圆产能和内存分配——是 AI 增长的主要瓶颈，而非需求。

"If we were to shut it off, it's like being in the 1990s, you've got internet and you're now saying, you know, only internet between the hours of two and four. After that, go to the library that we have down the hall... there'd be anarchy."

"如果我们把它关掉，就像回到了 1990 年代，你有了互联网，但现在规定只能在下午两点到四点上网。之后，你得去楼下走廊的图书馆……那将是一片混乱。"
