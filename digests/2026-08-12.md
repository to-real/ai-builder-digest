## Anthropic Engineering: How we contain Claude across products

**Anthropic reveals that environmental containment, not just model alignment, is the only reliable defense against sophisticated agent exploits.**

Anthropic detailed their multi-layered containment strategy for Claude across different products, ranging from ephemeral containers for `claude.ai` to local VMs for Claude Cowork. The post highlights that while model-layer defenses are improving, they are probabilistic and fallible; the deterministic boundaries of the environment—like gVisor containers and hypervisors—are what truly cap the blast radius. Key lessons learned include treating project configurations as untrusted input until verified, recognizing that users themselves can be injection vectors, and understanding that egress controls are critical since data often escapes through permitted paths like approved API domains.

Anthropic 详细介绍了他们在不同产品中针对 Claude 的多层防护策略，从 `claude.ai` 的临时容器到 Claude Cowork 的本地 VM。文章强调，虽然模型层的防御在改进，但它们是概率性的且容易失效；环境的确定性边界——如 gVisor 容器和虚拟机监控程序——才是真正限制爆炸半径的关键。吸取的主要教训包括：在验证之前将项目配置视为不受信任的输入，认识到用户本身可能成为注入向量，以及理解出站控制至关重要，因为数据通常会通过批准的 API 域名等许可路径泄露。

## No Priors — Building an Autonomous Enterprise for Real-World Services with Netic Founder Melisa Tokmak

*No Priors*

**Real-world AI adoption is happening fastest in unsexy essential services, where operational complexity makes autonomous agents a necessity rather than a luxury.**

Melisa Tokmak, founder and CEO of Netic, explains how her company builds AI to run essential service businesses like HVAC, plumbing, and pet care. Unlike AI rollups that rely on M&A, Netic focuses on building a product layer that handles complex operational workflows—from customer intake to labor dispatch—allowing enterprises to focus on service delivery. Tokmak argues that while big labs solve generalizable problems, the "last mile" of execution in these industries requires deep, specific orchestration that labs ignore. She notes that over 70% of their enterprise clients' customers now interact first with an AI agent, proving that these "old school" industries are actually tech-forward when value is clear. Her vision is an "autonomous enterprise" where AI handles everything except the actual labor, and she emphasizes hiring for agency and long-term commitment over the "permanent underclass" mindset of quick exits.

Netic 创始人兼 CEO Melisa Tokmak 解释了她的公司如何构建 AI 来运营暖通空调、管道维护和宠物护理等基础服务业务。与依赖并购的 AI 整合公司不同，Netic 专注于构建产品层，处理从客户接入到人工调度的复杂运营工作流，使企业能够专注于服务交付。Tokmak 认为，虽然大实验室解决的是通用问题，但这些行业中的“最后一公里”执行需要实验室所忽视的深度、特定的编排。她指出，超过 70% 的企业客户现在首先与 AI 代理互动，证明这些“老派”行业在价值明确时实际上是技术领先的。她的愿景是“自主企业”，AI 处理除实际劳动之外的所有事务，并强调在招聘时看重主观能动性和长期承诺，而非那种追求快速退出的“永久底层”心态。
