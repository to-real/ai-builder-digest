## No Priors — Superintelligence Needs the Physical World with CEO and Co-Founder of Arda Bob McGrew

*No Priors*

**Superintelligence requires transforming the physical world, and the "hard takeoff" RSI argument is wrong because researchers were never the bottleneck.**

Bob McGrew, former Chief Research Officer at OpenAI and early Palantir exec, now CEO and co-founder of Arda, argues that digital-only superintelligence is a trap. The common hard takeoff argument says: automate 90% of a researcher's job, go 10x faster; automate 99%, go 100x faster. McGrew calls this "straightforwardly wrong" because researchers were never the bottleneck. Even at OpenAI in 2024, the limiting factor was GPU compute, not researcher headcount. Automating researchers is a big one-time S-curve improvement, similar to the jump from GPT-4o to o3 with reasoning, but it does not create a runaway self-improvement loop. GPUs were maybe 35% optimally utilized; automation might push that to 90%, but the difference between 90% and 99% is marginal.

When intelligence becomes cheap, the complements to intelligence, perception and action, become scarce. You can have a "country of geniuses in a data center" but if they can only send emails, they are trapped. McGrew's thought experiment: to build more data centers you need bulldozers, to build a bulldozer factory you need to clear ground, and to clear ground you need more bulldozers. The physical world creates circular dependencies that pure digital intelligence cannot break.

Arda is tackling American manufacturing competitiveness. The core problem: 75% of iPhone parts change each year, and traditional automation is bespoke machinery that does not generalize between product generations. Arda partnered with Anduril to build a software platform where manufacturing engineers upload product designs and the system designs and reconfigures automated lines in seven days instead of months. Their first user completed a virtual build in 90 minutes instead of days. They are now deployed across all of Anduril's product divisions, working on everything from drones to missiles to the Fury collaborative combat aircraft.

McGrew chose not to train frontier models, using them instead as orchestrators over specialized algorithms and tools. He draws on the forward-deployed engineering model from Palantir: embed engineers with customers, find power users willing to fight through early pain, and productize what they learn. "There's not really any such thing as a very large organization," he says. "Big organizations are just an assemblage of very small teams."

Bob McGrew，前 OpenAI 首席研究官、Palantir 早期高管，现为 Arda 的 CEO 兼联合创始人，认为纯数字的超级智能是一个陷阱。常见的硬起飞论证是：自动化研究员 90% 的工作就能快 10 倍，自动化 99% 就快 100 倍。McGrew 认为这"根本就是错的"，因为研究员从来不是瓶颈。即使在 2024 年的 OpenAI，限制因素也是 GPU 算力而非研究员人数。自动化研究员是一次重大的 S 曲线提升，类似于从 GPT-4o 到带 reasoning 的 o3 的飞跃，但不会创造失控的自我改进循环。GPU 可能只达到了 35% 的最优利用率，自动化或许能推到 90%，但从 90% 到 99% 的差异其实很小。

当智能变得廉价时，智能的互补品，即感知和行动，就变得稀缺。你可以拥有"数据中心里的天才之国"，但如果他们只能发邮件，那他们就被困住了。McGrew 的思想实验：要建更多数据中心需要推土机，要建推土机工厂需要平整土地，而平整土地需要更多推土机。物理世界创造了纯数字智能无法打破的循环依赖。

Arda 正在解决美国制造业竞争力问题。核心问题是：iPhone 每年 75% 的零件都会变化，而传统自动化是定制机器，无法在不同产品代际间复用。Arda 与 Anduril 合作构建了软件平台，制造工程师上传产品设计后，系统能在七天内设计并重新配置自动化产线，而非数月。他们的第一位用户在 90 分钟内完成了原本需要数天的虚拟构建。现在已部署到 Anduril 所有产品部门，从无人机到导弹再到 Fury 协同战斗机。

McGrew 选择不训练自己的 frontier model，而是将其作为编排器来调度专门算法和工具。他借鉴了 Palantir 的 forward-deployed engineering 模式：将工程师嵌入客户现场，找到愿意忍受早期痛苦的核心用户，并将学到的东西产品化。"其实没有所谓的大型组织，"他说，"大组织不过是一个个小团队的集合。"
