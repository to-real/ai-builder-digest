## Rich Sutton and Khurram Javed: Why AI Models Stop Learning, and How to Start It Again

*Training Data*

**True intelligence requires continual learning from the real world, not static pre-training on finite data or synthetic simulations.**

Rich Sutton, the father of reinforcement learning, argues that the current AI paradigm is fundamentally flawed because models stop learning after deployment. He introduces the "Big World Hypothesis," stating the world is infinitely more complex than any dataset, making synthetic data a "big mistake" that relies on human bottlenecks. Instead, he advocates for "continual deep learning"—algorithms that update weights in real-time without catastrophic forgetting, allowing agents to form their own abstractions and learn directly from experience. "I'm not weird. The field is weird," Sutton insists, noting that all learning should be continual by definition.

强化学习之父 Rich Sutton 认为，当前的 AI 范式存在根本性缺陷，因为模型在部署后就停止了学习。他提出了“大世界假说”，指出世界比任何数据集都要无限复杂，这使得依赖人类瓶颈的合成数据成为一个“巨大的错误”。相反，他提倡“持续深度学习”——即在没有灾难性遗忘的情况下实时更新权重的算法，允许智能体形成自己的抽象概念并直接从经验中学习。“我不奇怪，是这个领域奇怪，”Sutton 坚称，并指出所有的学习本质上都应该是持续的。
