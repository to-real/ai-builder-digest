## Latent Space — The Revolution vs The Renaissance — Thariq Shihipar, OpenAI

*Latent Space*

**The real bottleneck in AI building isn't model intelligence, it's the quality of your feedback loop with the model.**

Thariq Shihipar, who just announced his move to OpenAI after a stint at Anthropic, frames AI's impact as two simultaneous forces: an industrial revolution (cheaper, more reliable, scalable software) and a renaissance (personal, artistic, refined through human iteration). The industry over-indexed on the factory in 2026. The next wave is about putting the human back in the loop, not removing them.

刚宣布从 Anthropic 跳槽到 OpenAI 的 Thariq Shihipar 将 AI 的影响框架为两股同时发生的力量：工业革命（更便宜、更可靠、可规模化的软件）和文艺复兴（个性化、艺术性、通过人的迭代来精炼）。2026 年行业过度偏向了工厂模式，下一波浪潮要把人重新放回回路中，而不是把人移除。

He admits he was wrong about models needing to work in specific formats like Bash or XML. As models generalize, they adapt to whatever format humans naturally use, including drawing frontends directly in Figma. This means the future is collaborative, not alien. "The models as they get more general will just be able to work in whatever format you want."

他承认自己之前错了，以为模型只能用 Bash 或 XML 等特定格式工作。随着模型变得更通用，它们会适应人类自然使用的任何格式，包括直接在 Figma 里画前端。这意味着未来是协作式的，不是异化的。"随着模型变得更通用，它们就能用你想要的任何格式来工作。"

His most practical insight: slop happens because builders don't dogfood their own output. He created a "/human" folder with different linting rules (high comment density, no single-character variables, limited line length) that serves as both spec and real code, letting him play with and understand what the model generates rather than receiving opaque artifacts.

他最实用的洞察：slop 之所以产生，是因为构建者没有在自己的开发过程中试用产出。他创建了一个 "/human" 文件夹，使用不同的 lint 规则（高注释密度、禁止单字符变量名、限制行长度），既是规格说明也是真实代码，让他能把玩和理解模型生成的内容，而不是接收不透明的产物。

On decision models, he argues they're under-utilized and not necessarily dumber. After one forward pass, a model already knows much of what it needs. Decoding extra tokens is often wasteful. He sees decision models sitting between hard-coded logic and full inference loops, ideal for use cases like support bots where you need routing intelligence but not deep reasoning on every turn.

关于 decision model，他认为被低估了，而且不一定更笨。一次 forward pass 之后，模型已经知道了很多它需要的信息。解码额外的 token 往往是浪费。他认为 decision model 介于硬编码逻辑和完整推理循环之间，非常适合客服机器人这类需要路由智能但不需要每轮深度推理的场景。

His most memorable line: "I don't even really feel like I'm bottlenecked by intelligence maybe. Like, maybe I'm bottlenecked by ambition." The constraint isn't capability, it's having a big enough vision and better ways to work with the model. On RSI, he sees it turning fields like biology into knowledge work, where humans make decisions and allocate resources while AI handles technical execution, potentially creating more white-collar jobs, not fewer.

他最令人印象深刻的金句："我甚至不觉得我被智能瓶颈了。也许我被野心瓶颈了。" 约束不在能力，而在是否有足够大的愿景和更好的与模型协作的方式。关于 RSI，他认为它会把生物学等领域转化为知识工作，人类做决策和资源分配，AI 处理技术执行，可能创造更多白领岗位，而非更少。
