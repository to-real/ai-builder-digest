## Training Data — David Silver and Lasse Espeholt of Ineffable Intelligence: A Superlearner for the Age of Experience

*Training Data*

**The path to superintelligence doesn't go through human data — it goes through systems that discover everything for themselves.**

David Silver (creator of AlphaGo and AlphaZero) and Lasse Espeholt (pioneer of scalable RL architectures like IMPALA and Seed RL) co-founded Ineffable Intelligence with a contrarian thesis: the LLM path, built on human data and human-in-the-loop training, cannot reach true superintelligence. Their bet is on "superlearners" — agents that learn purely from experience with simple reward signals in rich environments they call "crucibles." The AlphaGo-to-AlphaZero transition is their proof point: AlphaGo took two years of human-in-the-loop work to beat Lee Sedol; AlphaZero, once it dropped human data entirely, surpassed the best ever chess AI in four hours. Simple objectives in complex environments yield emergent complexity — just as evolution's simple fitness signal produced all of life's complexity, and humans' simple goals led to language, arithmetic, science, and technology in a cascading chain. Silver predicts strong evidence within a year and true superintelligence within three. On alignment, he argues language-based monitoring will fail as agents grow more powerful; instead, we need to build the science of superintelligence in simulation first — accelerate in simulation, pace carefully in the real world. They've secured the largest GPU cluster on GCP (Vera Rubin architecture) and keep the team at 20 people to maximize compute-per-person ratio.

David Silver（AlphaGo 和 AlphaZero 的创造者）与 Lasse Espeholt（IMPALA、Seed RL 等可扩展 RL 架构的先驱）共同创立了 Ineffable Intelligence，核心论点是：基于人类数据和人在回路训练的 LLM 路径无法通向真正的超级智能。他们押注"superlearner"——纯粹从经验中学习的 agent，在被称为"crucible"的丰富环境中用简单奖励信号驱动。AlphaGo 到 AlphaZero 的转变就是证据：AlphaGo 花两年人在回路才击败李世乭；AlphaZero 抛弃人类数据后，四小时就超越了史上最强国际象棋 AI。简单目标在复杂环境中产生涌现复杂性——正如进化的简单适应度信号产生了生命的全部复杂性，人类的简单目标在级联链条中催生了语言、算术、科学和技术。Silver 预言一年内有强证据、三年内实现真正的超级智能。关于对齐，他认为基于语言的监控在 agent 越来越强时会失效；正确做法是先在模拟中建立超级智能的科学——模拟中加速，真实世界谨慎推进。他们已拿到 GCP 上最大的 GPU 集群（Vera Rubin 架构），团队控制在 20 人以最大化人均算力比。
