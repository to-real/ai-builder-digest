## Aaron Levie

*CEO, Box*

**Coding is the preview of what happens to all knowledge work: humans shift from doing to coordinating agents, then to setting goals agents optimize against.**

Levie laid out a three-stage trajectory for knowledge work. First, humans move from manual research and content creation to coordinating agent work. Next, as coordination itself becomes a bottleneck, humans give agents goals to optimize against and agents solve increasingly large problems autonomously. He argued this won't stay confined to coding: manufacturing, architecture, life sciences, material sciences, and cybersecurity will all converge toward this form factor because agents can use computers, leverage existing tools, write new ones, and generate code on the fly. The remaining hurdle is that much knowledge work is non-verifiable, keeping humans in the loop longer than in coding, but the handoff zone grows continuously.

Levie 描绘了知识工作的三阶段演进。第一阶段，人类从手动研究和内容创作转向协调 agent 工作。第二阶段，当协调本身成为瓶颈时，人类给 agent 设定优化目标，agent 自主解决越来越大的问题。他认为这不会局限于编程：制造业、建筑、生命科学、材料科学和网络安全都将向这一形态收敛，因为 agent 能使用计算机、调用现有工具、编写新工具并即时生成代码。剩余障碍在于大量知识工作仍不可验证，人类介入的时间会比编程领域更长，但可交接的部分会持续扩大。

## Swyx

Swyx warned against reducing models to two-dimensional Pareto comparisons of cost versus quality. The real bottleneck is human attention: a cheaper model that fails silently or requires constant supervision costs more in human time than a pricier model that works reliably. Pareto charts that make every model reducible to two numbers can mask severe reliability problems that only surface in production.

Swyx 警告不要将模型简化为成本与质量的二维 Pareto 比较。真正的瓶颈是人类注意力：一个更便宜但静默失败或需要持续监督的模型，在人类时间上的成本远高于一个更贵但可靠运行的模型。将每个模型简化为两个数字的 Pareto 图表可能掩盖严重的可靠性问题，这些问题只在生产环境中才暴露。

## Training Data — OpenAI's Noam Brown: Why Game Theory Didn't Lead to General Intelligence (Live at No Limit Finals)

*Training Data*

**Game theory's core assumption that all players are rational was the wrong foundation for general intelligence; training on human data and scaling test-time reasoning was right.**

Noam Brown, reasoning researcher at OpenAI who built the world's first superhuman poker AI and then the diplomacy AI Cicero, spent a decade believing computational game theory was the road to AGI. Then diplomacy broke his faith. Game theory assumes all players are rational and that rationality is common knowledge. Humans are not rational. When his diplomacy bot applied game-theoretic strategies, it made moves that were technically "optimal" but practically absurd, like offering a penny in the ultimatum game and expecting acceptance. The breakthrough came from abandoning rationality assumptions entirely: train models to imitate human behavior, then optimize against that simulation of human irrationality.

This "crisis of faith" led Brown to join OpenAI in 2022, when most of the field was betting on scaling pre-training. He argued that GPT-4 couldn't play Go or poker competently, and scaling pre-training alone would require roughly six orders of magnitude more compute to get there, which was unrealistic. The key unlock was reinforcement learning on chain-of-thought reasoning: models that think longer before responding perform better, and this scales test-time compute in a general-purpose way across any domain.

Brown calls the current era the "centaur period," where human plus AI outperforms either alone. Models are "jagged" — superhuman at some tasks, surprisingly bad at others, like spending hours optimizing numerics that don't matter. His most provocative claim: "if somebody is a promising student, the best thing to do is actually to drop out and join the labs now." He gives AI a 20% chance of beating all humans at the World Series of Poker within five years, but only if it can read people's faces to detect bluffs.

Noam Brown 是 OpenAI 的推理研究员，曾构建世界上首个超人扑克 AI 和外交 AI Cicero。他花了十年时间相信计算博弈论是通往 AGI 的道路，直到外交研究打破了他的信念。博弈论假设所有玩家都是理性的，且理性是共识。但人类并不理性。当他的外交 bot 应用博弈论策略时，做出了技术上"最优"但实际上荒谬的举动，比如在最后通牒博弈中只给对方一分钱并期望对方接受。突破来自于完全放弃理性假设：训练模型模仿人类行为，然后针对这个人类非理性模拟进行优化。

这次"信仰危机"促使 Brown 在 2022 年加入 OpenAI，当时业界大多数人押注扩大预训练规模。他指出 GPT-4 无法胜任围棋或扑克，仅靠扩大预训练需要大约六个数量级的算力提升才能达到目标，这不现实。关键突破在于对 chain-of-thought 推理进行强化学习：模型在回答前思考越久表现越好，这以一种通用方式扩展了测试时算力，适用于任何领域。

Brown 将当前时代称为"半人马时期"，人加 AI 的组合优于任何一方单独行动。模型是"锯齿状"的：在某些任务上超人，在其他任务上却出奇地差，比如花几个小时优化根本不重要的数值精度。他最具争议的观点是："如果一个人是有前途的学生，最好的选择其实是退学，现在就加入实验室。"他认为 AI 在五年内打败所有人类赢得世界扑克系列赛的概率约为 20%，但前提是它能读懂人脸来识别虚张声势。
