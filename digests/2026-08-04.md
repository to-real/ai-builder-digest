## Training Data — Building the Automated AGI Lab: Core Automation's Jerry Tworek and Rohan Anil

*Training Data*

**Transformers are hitting a ceiling because they can't learn continuously at test time; the next leap requires new architectures and fully automated research labs.**

Transformer 正在触及天花板，因为它们无法在测试时持续学习；下一次飞跃需要全新的架构和全自动化的研究实验室。

Jerry Tworek and Rohan Anil, founders of Core Automation and former leads at OpenAI and Google respectively, argue that while transformers are economically valuable, they fail at real-world adaptation because they stop learning after training. Jerry defines AGI not as outperforming humans, but as a model that can improve itself without human intervention. Rohan adds that current inference methods (one token at a time) are computationally inefficient and that kernel generation is a major bottleneck for discovering new architectures. Their mission is to build the most automated lab possible to experiment at the speed required to find the transformer successor.

Core Automation 的联合创始人、前 OpenAI 和 Google 负责人 Jerry Tworek 和 Rohan Anil 认为，虽然 Transformer 具有经济价值，但它们在现实世界适应性方面失败了，因为它们在训练后就停止了学习。Jerry 将 AGI 定义的不是超越人类，而是一个可以在没有人类干预的情况下自我改进的模型。Rohan 补充说，当前的推理方法（一次一个 token）在计算上效率低下，而内核生成是发现新架构的主要瓶颈。他们的使命是建立尽可能自动化的实验室，以找到 Transformer 继任者所需的速度进行实验。
