## No Priors — From Restoring Sight to Reimagining the Brain, with Max Hodak

*No Priors*

**The brain is literally a computer, and we can engineer it to restore senses like vision.**

Max Hodak, founder of Science Corp, argues that the brain is unambiguously a computer that processes inputs through a limited set of "wires" like the optic nerve. His company's Prima retinal prosthesis, which recently received CE marking in Europe, validates this approach by using a chip implanted under the retina and laser-projecting glasses to restore form vision to blind patients. Hodak distinguishes between "hand substitute" BCIs—like silent speech interfaces—and the deeper goal of substrate independence, where we upgrade the brain's input/output channels to achieve vision, hearing, and motor control. He points to the "platonic representation hypothesis," noting that the internal geometry of AI models increasingly mirrors biological brains, suggesting both are converging on the same underlying data manifold. "If you apply enough compute to matter, you get this thing that looks like intelligence," Hodak observes, framing the challenge not as discovering drugs but as engineering interfaces to the brain's computational structure.

Science Corp 创始人 Max Hodak 认为，大脑毫无疑问是一台计算机，通过视神经等有限的“线路”处理输入。他公司的 Prima 视网膜假体最近获得了欧洲 CE 认证，通过在视网膜下植入芯片并使用激光投影眼镜，向盲患者的大脑发送信号以恢复形式视觉，从而验证了这一方法。Hodak 区分了“手部替代”BCI（如无声语音接口）与“基质独立性”这一更深层次的目标，即升级大脑的输入/输出通道以实现视觉、听觉和运动控制。他指出了“柏拉图表示假说”，指出 AI 模型的内部几何结构越来越像生物大脑，表明两者正收敛于相同的底层数据流形。“如果你对物质施加足够的算力，你就会得到这种看起来像智能的东西，”Hodak 观察道，他将挑战不定义为发现药物，而是定义为针对大脑计算结构的工程接口。
