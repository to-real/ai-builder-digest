## The MAD Podcast — Jev: The AI That Makes Decisions, Not Text

*The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck*

**AI's automation bottleneck is reliability, not intelligence. Jev outputs decisions instead of text, making AI composable inside software like logic gates.**

Diogo Almeida, CEO of TypeSafe and co-inventor of RLHF at OpenAI, spent two years in stealth building Jev, a "system 1" model that returns probabilities instead of prose. His core diagnosis: LLMs are "95% Einstein, 5% Mr. Bean," and if they can't tell you when they're being Mr. Bean, you can't automate anything. RLHF trained models to please humans, introducing sycophancy, over-refusal, and stylistic repetition. RLVR crushes benchmarks but doesn't deliver reliable automation. TypeSafe's new training method, RLCD, optimizes for machine-native intelligence: classifiers you can compose like logic gates inside software, with fine-grained probability thresholds developers control.

Almeida calls classification "AI-complete" and being called a classifier "a compliment." The copycats that emerged within weeks copied the interface but missed the years of work making intelligence reliable. He's skeptical of recursive self-improvement ("people don't understand diminishing returns in the real world") and dismisses pre-training as "a giant dumpster fire of wasting resources." The real metric: intelligence per dollar, not intelligence per second. As cost drops, Jevons paradox kicks in and demand for machine intelligence will explode like semiconductors did. "We're building prod, not God."

Diogo Almeida 是 TypeSafe 的 CEO，也是 OpenAI RLHF 的联合发明人，花了两年时间隐身打造 Jev，一种返回概率而非散文的"系统 1"模型。他的核心诊断：LLM 是"95% 爱因斯坦、5% 憨豆先生"，如果它不能告诉你什么时候是憨豆先生，你就无法自动化任何事。RLHF 训练模型取悦人类，引入了谄媚、过度拒绝和风格重复。RLVR 刷爆基准测试但无法交付可靠的自动化。TypeSafe 的新训练方法 RLCD 优化机器原生智能：开发者可控的、可像逻辑门一样在软件中组合的分类器。

Almeida 称分类是"AI 完备的"，被叫作分类器是"一种赞美"。几周内涌现的模仿者复制了接口，但错过了让智能变得可靠的多年工作。他对递归自我改进持怀疑态度（"人们不理解现实世界的边际收益递减"），并把预训练斥为"浪费资源的巨型垃圾场"。真正的指标：每美元智能，不是每秒智能。成本下降后，Jevons 悖论生效，对机器智能的需求将像半导体一样爆发。"我们在建产品，不是造神。"
