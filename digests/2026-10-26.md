## The MAD Podcast — Jev: The AI That Makes Decisions, Not Text

*The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck*

**AI's bottleneck is reliability, not intelligence — and a new model architecture that outputs decisions instead of text could make intelligence as composable and ubiquitous as software itself.**

Diogo Almeida, CEO of TypeSafe and co-inventor of RLHF at OpenAI, argues that RLHF made models stylistically pleasing but practically unreliable for automation. His creation JEV is a "system one" model that returns calibrated probabilities instead of autoregressive text — making it fast, cheap, and reliable enough to embed inside software like a logic gate. The key insight: you don't ask JEV "should I escalate?" in one shot; you decompose into dozens of small, legible classifiers (is this inbound? is this from an investor?) that each return a score developers can threshold themselves. This composability — reliable building blocks stacked and replicated like software — is what enables "intelligence everywhere," from every database row to every code branch. JEV's training method (RLCD) optimizes for machine-native intelligence, not human-pleasing chat. Almeida calls pretraining "a giant dumpster fire of wasting resources" and argues classification is AI-complete, not narrow. On copycats: "I literally thought it would take a week" to build what they're shipping, "but it was so unbelievably dumb that it was embarrassing" — the hard part is reliability, not the interface. He doesn't believe in recursive self-improvement (RSI) due to diminishing returns, but does believe AI winter from useless automation is now averted. As he puts it: "95% Einstein, 5% Mr. Bean, and if it doesn't tell you when it's the Mr. Bean, then how are you gonna automate anything?"

Diogo Almeida 是 TypeSafe CEO，也是 RLHF 在 OpenAI 的共同发明人。他认为 RLHF 让模型在风格上讨喜，但在自动化上不可靠。他创造的 JEV 是一种"system one"模型，返回校准后的概率而非自回归文本——快速、廉价、足够可靠，可以像逻辑门一样嵌入软件。核心洞察：不是一次问 JEV"要不要升级"，而是分解成几十个可读的小分类器（是否 inbound？是否来自投资者？），每个返回开发者可自行设阈值的分数。这种可组合性——像软件一样堆叠和复制可靠构件——让"智能无处不在"成为可能，从数据库每一行到代码每一分支。JEV 的训练方法 RLCD 优化的是机器原生智能，而非讨人喜欢的对话。Almeida 称预训练是"浪费资源的巨型垃圾堆"，并论证分类是 AI 完备的、而非狭隘的。关于模仿者："我一开始真以为一周就能做出他们现在发布的东西，但结果蠢得令人尴尬"——难点是可靠性，不是接口。他不相信递归自我改进（RSI），因为收益递减；但他认为因自动化无用而导致的 AI 寒冬现在已经避免。正如他所说："95% 爱因斯坦，5% 憨豆先生，如果它不告诉你什么时候是憨豆先生，你怎么自动化任何东西？"
