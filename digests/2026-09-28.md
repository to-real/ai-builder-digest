## No Priors — Why Diffusion Will Win AI Inference with Inception Co-Founder and CEO Stefano Ermon

*No Priors*

**Diffusion-based LLMs will win on inference economics because parallelism beats sequential generation — the same lesson that killed RNNs.**

Stefano Ermon, Stanford professor and co-inventor of diffusion models (2019, with Yang Song), founded Inception to scale diffusion-based language models to commercial grade. His core argument: autoregressive models are fundamentally memory-bound at inference — generating one token at a time, spending most compute moving weights across memory hierarchy with very little arithmetic. Diffusion models output many tokens simultaneously, mapping naturally to GPU parallelism. "The bitter lesson is that the more parallel solution is the one that is eventually going to win."

Inception's Mercury models already match the quality of speed-optimized frontier models (Groq flash, OpenAI mini/nano) while being significantly faster. They're serving real customers today — voice agent company OpenCall switched from Cerebras custom silicon to Inception's diffusion LLMs on standard NVIDIA GPUs, getting the same speed with more hardware availability and lower cost. Ermon estimates 20-30% of workloads are latency-critical, forming the addressable market.

Beyond speed, diffusion models offer structural advantages: coarse-to-fine generation enables early constraint evaluation and steering (impossible with autoregressive models that must complete before scoring), and evidence suggests better data efficiency since denoising implicitly augments training data. The main challenge is an immature ecosystem — Inception had to build its own serving engine, SFT, and RLHF stacks from scratch.

Stefano Ermon 是斯坦福教授、diffusion 模型的共同发明人（2019 年与 Yang Song），创立 Inception 将基于 diffusion 的语言模型推向商用。核心论点：自回归模型推理时从根本上受限于内存带宽——逐 token 生成，大部分算力花在权重搬运上，实际计算极少。Diffusion 模型同时输出多个 token，天然适配 GPU 并行。"The bitter lesson is that the more parallel solution is the one that is eventually going to win."

Inception 的 Mercury 模型已在质量上追平前沿实验室的速度优化模型（Groq flash、OpenAI mini/nano），同时显著更快。已在服务真实客户——语音 agent 公司 OpenCall 从 Cerebras 专用芯片切到 Inception 的 diffusion LLM 跑在标准 NVIDIA GPU 上，同等速度、更多硬件可用性、更低成本。Ermon 估计 20-30% 的工作负载对延迟敏感，构成可触达市场。

超越速度，diffusion 模型还有结构性优势：由粗到细的生成允许早期约束评估和引导（自回归模型必须生成完毕才能评分），且有证据表明数据效率更高（去噪隐式做数据增强）。主要挑战是生态不成熟——Inception 不得不从零自建 serving engine、SFT 和 RLHF 全套基础设施。
