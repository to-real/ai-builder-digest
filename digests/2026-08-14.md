**Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands**

Anthropic introduces Managed Agents, a system architecture that separates the agent's reasoning (brain) from execution (hands) to improve reliability, security, and scalability.

Anthropic 推出了 Managed Agents，这是一种将代理的推理（大脑）与执行（双手）分离的系统架构，以提高可靠性、安全性和可扩展性。

The post explains how virtualizing agent components—session, harness, and sandbox—allows them to fail or be replaced independently. This decoupling improved time-to-first-token by 60% (p50) and 90% (p95), and enables secure credential management by keeping tokens out of the sandbox.

文章解释了如何通过虚拟化代理组件——session、harness 和 sandbox——使它们能够独立故障或替换。这种解耦将首字延迟时间（TTFT）缩短了 60%（p50）和 90%（p95），并通过将 token 保持在沙盒之外来实现安全的凭证管理。
