## The MAD Podcast — Who Feeds the GPUs? Inside AI's Hidden $30B Layer

*The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck*

**The software infrastructure layer between hardware and models is AI's most underrated moat — and VAST Data owns it.**

Renen Hallak, founder and CEO of VAST Data (valued at $30B), occupies the middle of Jensen Huang's "five layer cake" — the software infrastructure layer that manages compute, storage, networking, databases, and orchestration for AI. His core insight: the old shared-nothing architecture breaks at AI scale. VAST built "disaggregated shared everything" (DASE), where all nodes see all data as if locally attached via NVMe over Fabrics, enabling single clusters spanning exabytes with tens of thousands of nodes. The demand acceleration is staggering: one AI cloud customer planned for 500 petabytes over three years, then came back a quarter later needing an extra 2 exabytes on top. Hallak sees AI factories becoming essential for every enterprise — not just big labs — because "we infer during the day, fine-tune at night," and each organization's IP will distill into its own model weights. VAST just announced DataEnclave, leveraging Nvidia's encrypted memory for confidential computing: enterprises run inference on-prem without exposing their data, and model builders' weights stay encrypted end-to-end. Hallak's worldview: "I think in the next ten years, we'll see more difference than we did in the last thousand years." On competition: VAST has zero churn, is profitable, and benefits from the unsexiness of infrastructure — "storage was where startups go to die" kept competitors away.

Renen Hallak 是 VAST Data（估值 300 亿美元）的创始人兼 CEO，占据 Jensen Huang"五层蛋糕"的中间层——管理 AI 计算、存储、网络、数据库和编排的软件基础设施层。核心洞察：旧的 shared-nothing 架构在 AI 规模下会崩溃。VAST 构建了"disaggregated shared everything"（DASE），所有节点通过 NVMe over Fabrics 像访问本地数据一样访问全局数据，单集群可跨 EB 级、数万节点。需求加速令人震惊：一家 AI 云客户原计划三年 500PB，一个季度后追加了 2EB。Hallak 认为 AI 工厂将成为每家企业的必需品——"白天推理，夜间微调"，每家组织的 IP 会蒸馏为自己的模型权重。VAST 刚发布 DataEnclave，利用 Nvidia 加密内存实现机密计算：企业在本地运行推理不暴露数据，模型权重端到端加密。Hallak 的世界观："未来十年的变化将超过过去一千年。"关于竞争：VAST 零流失、已盈利，基础设施的不性感反而是护城河——"存储是创业公司的坟场"挡住了竞争者。
