## AI & I by Every — Why the Next Hit AI Product Will Be Social (Best of the Pod)

*AI & I by Every*

**The next massive AI product will be social and multiplayer, not just a technical marvel.**

Sarah Tavel, a Partner at Benchmark and early Pinterest employee, analyzes the evolution of consumer technology from technical depth to product genius. She argues that while early AI winners like ChatGPT and Character.AI resemble Google—deeply technical and "single player"—the next wave will mirror Instagram or Snap. These future products will be multiplayer experiences where users share prompts and agents, creating network effects. Tavel notes that current "Custom GPTs" lack the social trust and status-seeking mechanics necessary for a true community. She envisions a platform where "people who are really good" at AI make it accessible for the rest, similar to how Reddit currently serves as a hidden prompt library. "It shouldn't be this hard," she says, arguing that the best interfaces will let us learn from each other rather than forcing everyone to be a power user.

下一个巨大的 AI 产品将是社交和多人互动的，而不仅仅是技术奇迹。Benchmark 合伙人、Pinterest 早期员工 Sarah Tavel 分析了消费技术从技术深度向产品天才的演变。她认为，虽然 ChatGPT 和 Character.AI 等早期的 AI 赢家像 Google 一样——技术深厚且是“单机模式”——但下一波浪潮将像 Instagram 或 Snap。这些未来的产品将是多人互动体验，用户可以在其中分享 prompt 和 agent，创造网络效应。Tavel 指出，目前的“Custom GPTs”缺乏真正社区所需的社会信任和地位追求机制。她设想了一个平台，让“真正擅长”AI 的人为其他人提供便利，就像 Reddit 目前作为隐藏的 prompt 库一样。“不应该这么难，”她说，并认为最好的界面将让我们互相学习，而不是强迫每个人都成为高级用户。
