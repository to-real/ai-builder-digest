## Latent Space — Building Legal Agents When You Can't See the Data

*Latent Space*

**Domain-specific AI wins by owning the harness under extreme privacy constraints — Legora built a legal AI empire without ever seeing a single customer trace.**

Jacob Lauritzen, CTO of Legora (YC W24), runs one of the fastest-growing legal tech companies in history from Stockholm. The core constraint that shapes everything: Legora cannot look at any customer data because it belongs to law firm clients, not the firms themselves. They build evals by approximating what they think users are doing, and even synthetic data generation is severely limited. Despite this, they've built a product that handles 60% of legal work today — from research and drafting to their killer feature Tabular Review, which bulk-extracts across thousands of documents in spreadsheet format (up to a million cells/day). Even Claude for Legal adopted their terminology.

Model routing is baked in: lawyers don't care which model runs — "just make it work, my hourly rate is $1,000." They fought adding a model picker but eventually caved. Their evals team (3 engineers + 3 lawyers, plus a pool of "legal engineers" — Big Law lawyers with CS degrees, their hardest hire) tracks a heatmap across practice areas and tasks, and they've found that model releases often improve some tasks while regressing others — more than you'd think. All infrastructure runs on Azure because law firms initially refused AWS entirely. They're now starting to post-train open weight models for bounded tasks like legal research (fan-out retrieval), and exploring whether the agent itself can serve as the API. The partnership model is under pressure: firms need to codify human expertise into systems to build durable capital, but partners have no incentive to do so since they lose access when they leave.

领域特定的 AI 靠在极端隐私约束下掌控 harness 取胜——Legora 在从未看过任何客户 trace 的情况下建起了法律 AI 帝国。Jacob Lauritzen 是 Legora (YC W24) 的 CTO，从斯德哥尔摩运营着历史上增长最快的法律科技公司之一。核心约束：Legora 不能看任何客户数据，因为数据属于律所的客户而非律所本身。他们靠近似模拟用户行为来建 eval，合成数据生成也严重受限。尽管如此，他们已能处理 60% 的法律工作——从研究和起草到杀手级功能 Tabular Review（以表格格式批量提取数千份文档，每天可达百万单元格）。连 Claude for Legal 都采用了他们的术语。Model routing 内置在产品中：律师不在乎哪个模型在跑——"只要能用就行，我时薪 $1000。"他们最初抗拒加模型选择器但最终妥协。evals 团队（3 工程师 + 3 律师，加上"legal engineers"池子——有 CS 学位的大所律师，最难招的人）跨业务领域和任务追踪热力图，发现模型发布经常改善某些任务同时让其他任务退步——比你想的更频繁。全部基础设施跑在 Azure 上，因为律所最初完全拒绝 AWS。他们现在开始对开放权重模型做 post-training，用于法律检索等有界任务。合伙模式承压：律所需要把人类专长编码成系统来积累持久资本，但合伙人没有动力这么做——离开时就失去了访问权。
