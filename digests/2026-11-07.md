## Unsupervised Learning — Ep 97: Jev Creator on Decision Models, Benchmark Fatigue & Why the Labs Missed It

*Unsupervised Learning*

**The future of AI is machine-native, and the entire industry is building for the wrong consumer.**

Jogo, CEO of TypeSave and creator of JEV models, argues that nearly all LLM calls should ultimately be for computer consumption, not human consumption. Computers operate on bits, not strings, yet the entire LLM paradigm optimizes for human-pleasing outputs. This mismatch is why AI can solve Millennium Prize math problems but cannot automate accounting. JEV models are explicitly "system one" models: fast, cheap, and honest about their jaggedness. Jogo calls benchmark culture "pure overpromised, underdelivered" and says developer goodwill is burning faster than expected because every new model release optimizes for demos, not reliability. The KV cache is the "tyranny" constraining coding agents: it forces append-only state, prevents effective routing and sub-agent delegation, and blocks shared context between parallel agents. Jogo wants a future where agents share state, compress context semantically based on task ROI, and run MapReduce over conversation history. He estimates coding agents deliver a 20-40% speedup on average, and warns that Anthropic's valuation may reflect misunderstandings of AI's actual trajectory: "the 'let them cure cancer' is going to be like Marie Antoinette's 'let them eat cake.'" On AI safety, he believes ASI concerns are divorced from technological reality and that we are at least two gigantic breakthroughs away from true agency.

Jogo 是 TypeSave 的 CEO、JEV 模型的创造者，他认为几乎所有 LLM 调用最终都应该面向计算机消费而非人类消费。计算机操作的是比特而非字符串，但整个 LLM 范式却在优化人类喜好的输出。这种错配解释了为什么 AI 能解决千禧年数学奖问题却无法自动化会计。JEV 模型明确是"系统一"模型：快速、廉价、对自身的不平整性诚实。Jogo 称 benchmark 文化是"纯粹的过度承诺、交付不足"，并表示开发者的信任正在以超预期速度被消耗，因为每次新模型发布都在优化 demo 而非可靠性。KV cache 是 coding agent 的"专政"：它强制只追加状态、阻止有效路由和子 agent 委派、阻塞并行 agent 间的共享上下文。Jogo 希望未来的 agent 能共享状态、基于任务 ROI 语义压缩上下文、对对话历史做 MapReduce。他估计 coding agent 平均带来 20-40% 的提速，并警告 Anthropic 的估值可能反映了对 AI 实际轨迹的误解："'让他们治愈癌症'终将像玛丽·安托瓦内特的'让他们吃蛋糕'。"关于 AI 安全，他认为 ASI 担忧脱离了技术现实，距离真正的自主性至少还差两个巨大突破。
