## Training Data — Chai Discovery's Bitter Lesson: Drug Design Is Another Scaling Problem

*Training Data*

**Drug discovery is shifting from trial-and-error fishing to an engineering discipline where scaling laws and simplicity drive success.**

Josh and Matt, co-founders of Chai Discovery, argue that biology is undergoing a transformation similar to software engineering, where drug discovery is becoming a design problem rather than a search problem. They discuss how their "bitter lesson" philosophy—prioritizing scaling data, compute, and model simplicity over bespoke biological complexity—allowed them to jump antibody design success rates from 0.1% to 15%. By treating protein sequences as prompts and leveraging diffusion models, they've created a system where the model generates molecules with specific therapeutic properties, effectively turning the lab into a verification layer. They emphasize that as intelligence becomes commoditized, the competitive advantage lies in building robust infrastructure and maintaining rigorous scientific standards to partner with major pharma companies.

Chai Discovery 的联合创始人 Josh 和 Matt 认为，生物学正在经历类似于软件工程的变革，药物发现正从搜索问题转变为设计问题。他们讨论了他们的“苦涩教训”哲学——优先考虑数据、计算和模型简洁性的扩展，而不是定制的生物复杂性——这使他们将抗体设计的成功率从 0.1% 提高到了 15%。通过将蛋白质序列视为提示词并利用扩散模型，他们创建了一个系统，让模型生成具有特定治疗特性的分子，有效地将实验室变成了验证层。他们强调，随着智能变得商品化，竞争优势在于构建强大的基础设施并保持严格的科学标准，以便与大型制药公司合作。
