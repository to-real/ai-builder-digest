## Claude Blog: Claude in Chrome is generally available

**Claude can now act autonomously in your browser with prompt injection defenses that stopped 100% of red-team attacks on Sonnet 5 and Opus 5.**

Claude in Chrome is GA on every paid plan. Claude can view pages and take actions (click, type, navigate, fill forms) using your existing logins, accessing internal dashboards and legacy systems that lack API integrations. The key safety architecture: probes scan web content for prompt injections before Claude acts, and a safety classifier verifies each action matches the user's request before execution. On their strongest evaluation with professional red-teamers, no attacks succeeded against Sonnet 5 or Opus 5 with probes plus the automatic-approval classifier. Opus 4.5 saw a 17.6% attack success rate without safeguards. As the blog states: "When a probe detects a likely attack, Claude is warned to treat the content with suspicion and, if needed, to check with you before taking an action."

Claude in Chrome 在所有付费计划上正式开放。Claude 可以用你现有的登录查看页面并执行操作（点击、输入、导航、填表），访问没有 API 集成的内部看板和遗留系统。关键安全架构：probe 在 Claude 行动前扫描网页内容检测 prompt injection，安全分类器在执行前验证每个操作是否匹配用户请求。在专业红队最强评估中，Sonnet 5 和 Opus 5 在 probe 加自动审批分类器下零攻击成功。Opus 4.5 无防护时攻击成功率 17.6%。正如博客所说："当 probe 检测到可能的攻击时，Claude 会被警告以怀疑态度对待该内容，必要时会先跟你确认再行动。"
