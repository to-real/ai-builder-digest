## Aaron Levie

*CEO, Box*

**AI agents are creating a new class of "Frontier Data Engineers" because they require constant customization in undefined workflows.**

Levie explains that unlike traditional deterministic software, AI agents introduce non-determinism into workflows that have never been automated before. This means customers can't tell you what they want because the user journey doesn't exist yet, necessitating heavy customization, constant evals, and ongoing updates to keep agents aligned with business processes.

Levie 解释说，与传统的确定性软件不同，AI Agent 将非确定性引入了从未被自动化过的工作流中。这意味着客户无法告诉你他们想要什么，因为用户旅程尚不存在，这 necessitates 大量的定制、持续的评估和持续的更新，以保持 Agent 与业务流程的一致性。

## The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — The Biggest AI Deployment Nobody Talks About | Samsara CEO Sanjit Biswas

*The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck*

**The most significant AI deployment is happening in the physical world—construction, logistics, utilities—where digitizing messy reality creates massive value.**

Sanjit Biswas, CEO of Samsara, explains that "Physical AI" applies machine learning to the un-digitized infrastructure of our planet, from construction sites to the electrical grid. Unlike the digital world where data is abundant, the physical world requires hardware like GPS trackers and dashcams to capture 25 trillion data points annually, which Samsara uses to prevent 380,000 car crashes a year. Biswas notes a shift from the last two decades of mere data reporting to a new era where AI reasons over this data to take action, such as adjusting vehicle following distances based on weather or automating warranty claims. He envisions a future where robots handle repetitive tasks like road grading at night, while humans manage the messy exceptions, emphasizing that in physical operations, "risk is the opportunity" to make dangerous industries safer.

Samsara CEO Sanjit Biswas 解释说，“物理 AI” 将机器学习应用于我们星球上尚未数字化的基础设施，从建筑工地到电网。与数据丰富的数字世界不同，物理世界需要 GPS 追踪器和行车记录仪等硬件来每年捕获 25 万亿个数据点，Samsara 利用这些数据每年防止 38 万起车祸。Biswas 指出，过去二十年仅仅是数据报告，现在已转向一个新的时代，AI 对这些数据进行推理并采取行动，例如根据天气调整车辆跟车距离或自动化保修索赔。他设想了一个未来，机器人将在夜间处理道路平整等重复性任务，而人类则处理混乱的例外情况，并强调在物理运营中，“风险即机遇”，可以让危险行业变得更安全。
