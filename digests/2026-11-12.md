## Latent Space — Designing for Agents — Zeno Rocha, Resend

*Latent Space*

**Naivety was the best thing that ever happened to Resend — and AEO is the new SEO.**

Zeno Rocha, CEO of Resend, built a $10M ARR email infrastructure company with 4.3M users and 120K paid customers — starting with zero email knowledge. That naivety became the edge: Resend trusts users by default (unlike competitors who make you prove yourself first), then blocks quickly when something is off. The key insight is differentiating misuse from abuse — most blocked senders just have a "skill issue," not malicious intent.

On distribution, Rocha reveals that AEO now matters more than traditional SEO. Resend added llms.txt, .md versions of every page, and FAQ sections everywhere — because models can't execute JavaScript widgets, and recency bias in model recommendations favors tools with fresh organic mentions. When Rocha asked a model to compare Resend pricing to competitors, it got the numbers completely wrong; after pushing a .md version with plain-text pricing, the model got it right days later.

MCP usage is doubling every month (now 80+ tools). The first MCP was built by an a16z investor; building the remote version required implementing OAuth from scratch. Rocha's philosophy: "I would rather live in a reality where we're a $10M ARR company and my daughter wants to play with me than a $100M company where she's not interested in me." On infrastructure, Resend is now going "down to the metal" — building its own sending infrastructure instead of relying on AWS SES, to get better throughput and deliverability observability.

Zeno Rocha 是 Resend 的 CEO，从零邮件知识起步，建起了一家 $10M ARR、430 万用户、12 万付费客户的邮件基础设施公司。这种"无知"反而成了优势：Resend 默认信任用户（不像竞品让你先自证清白），发现异常后快速封禁。核心洞察是区分"误用"和"滥用"——大多数被封的发件人只是"技能问题"，并非恶意。

在分发上，Rocha 揭示 AEO 现在比传统 SEO 更重要。Resend 加了 llms.txt、每个页面的 .md 版本、以及全站 FAQ——因为模型无法执行 JavaScript 组件，而模型推荐中的时效性偏差偏向有新鲜有机提及的工具。当 Rocha 让模型比较 Resend 和竞品定价时，数字完全错了；推了 .md 纯文本定价版后，几天后模型就答对了。

MCP 使用量每月翻倍（现在 80+ 工具）。第一个 MCP 是 a16z 投资人建的；做远程版需要从零实现 OAuth。Rocha 的哲学："我宁愿活在 $10M ARR 且女儿愿意和我玩的世界，也不愿活在 $100M 但她对我没兴趣的世界。"基础设施方面，Resend 正在"下到金属层"——自建发送基础设施而非依赖 AWS SES，以获得更好的吞吐量和可投递性可观测性。
