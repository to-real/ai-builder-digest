## No Priors — The Changing Physics of Cybersecurity and Drug Development with OpenAI President Greg Brockman

*No Priors*

**AI has crossed the threshold from reproducing knowledge to generating genuinely new knowledge — and the implications for security, drug discovery, and how we work are seismic.**

Greg Brockman, cofounder and president of OpenAI, argues that their recent math results — resolving roughly 400 important open problems, including century-old ones — definitively prove AI can create new knowledge, not just remix existing ideas. The key insight isn't that machines give answers; it's what humans equipped with these tools can now achieve that was unthinkable before.

On drug discovery, Brockman makes a first-principles argument that's striking: humans are "incredibly bad at biology" because it's foreign to how we evolved, but neural nets have no inherent reason to be better at natural language than at biology. If LLMs cracked language, they should crack the language of biology too. The bottleneck is data, not capability — and regulatory filings alone are a huge pain point AI can immediately alleviate.

On cybersecurity, Brockman's boldest claim: "cyber spend, security spend should actually vastly outstrip software spend." As software becomes cheap commodity, the value shifts to software that's reliable and secure. OpenAI is practicing what it preaches — they pulled 25 production engineers off projects to focus entirely on security, used their frontier model to find and fix vulnerabilities, and have committed $1 billion to help frontline defenders. We're in a "defender's window" where peak AI capabilities can help secure systems before those capabilities diffuse to attackers.

On product, Brockman reveals that Dots — OpenAI's proactive, always-on agent — has changed how he works. It notices things he never asked it to notice, like calendar conflicts and forgotten tasks. His vision: "the best UI is no UI" — just an AI you can talk to across any surface, with generative UI created on the fly when needed.

As he puts it: "Technology is to lift up human civilization. It's the single most fundamental driver of improvement in quality of life. And that is what we should be seeking from AI."

Greg Brockman，OpenAI 联合创始人兼总裁，认为他们最近的数学成果——解决了大约 400 个重要的开放问题，包括百年难题——确凿证明了 AI 能创造新知识，而不只是重组已有想法。关键洞察不在于机器给出答案，而在于装备了这些工具的人类现在能做到以前不可想象的事。

关于药物发现，Brockman 从第一性原理出发提出了一个惊人论点：人类"在生物学上极其糟糕"，因为生物学与我们的进化方式格格不入，但神经网络没有内在理由在自然语言上比在生物学上更强。如果 LLM 攻克了语言，也应该能攻克生物学的语言。瓶颈是数据，不是能力——而监管申报本身就是 AI 可以立即缓解的巨大痛点。

关于网络安全，Brockman 最大胆的断言："安全支出应该远超软件支出。"当软件变成廉价商品，价值就转移到可靠且安全的软件上。OpenAI 正在以身作则——抽调 25 名生产工程师全力投入安全，用前沿模型发现和修复漏洞，并承诺投入 10 亿美元帮助一线防御者。我们正处于"防御者窗口期"，前沿 AI 能力可以在扩散到攻击者之前帮助加固系统。

关于产品，Brockman 透露 Dots——OpenAI 的主动式、始终在线的 agent——已经改变了他的工作方式。它会注意到他从未要求它注意的事，比如日历冲突和遗忘的任务。他的愿景："最好的 UI 就是没有 UI"——只是一个你可以在任何界面上与之对话的 AI，需要时即时生成 UI。

正如他所说："技术是为了提升人类文明。这是生活质量改善最根本的驱动力。这也是我们应该从 AI 中追求的。"
