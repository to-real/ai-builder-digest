## Unsupervised Learning — Ep 97: Jev Creator on Decision Models, Benchmark Fatigue & Why the Labs Missed It

*Unsupervised Learning*

**The future of AI is machine-native, and the field's obsession with benchmarks and human-pleasing outputs is blinding it to what actually matters: reliability.**

Jogo, CEO of TypeSave and creator of Jev, argues that the critical question is what percentage of LLM calls are meant for computer consumption versus human consumption — even the most "string-pilled" people admit it's many nines percent machine-native, it's just a question of when. Jev is built as a "system 1" model: honest about its weaknesses, optimized for discrete decisions that software needs, not human-pleasing prose. He calls the KV cache the "tyranny" constraining coding agents — it makes routing impossible, prevents subagents from sharing context, and forces append-only state like global variables passed around forever. His estimate: coding agents deliver a 20-40% speedup on average, and the field has Stockholm syndrome, accepting whatever model providers ship instead of collaborating on what's actually needed. On Anthropic's IPO: "The 'let them cure cancer' is going to be like Marie Antoinette's 'let them eat cake.'" On ASI safety: we're two gigantic breakthroughs away from true agency, and people who treat Bostrom's Superintelligence as reality over sci-fi worry him. His north star: "Why can we solve Millennium Prize problems in math but not automate anything touching accounting? That should be really weird to a lot of people."

Jogo（TypeSave CEO，Jev 创造者）认为关键问题是：LLM 调用中多大比例是给计算机消费的、多大比例是给人类的——即便最"字符串派"的人也承认绝大多数应该是机器原生的，只是时间问题。Jev 被设计为"系统1"模型：坦诚自身弱点，针对软件需要的离散决策优化，而非取悦人类的文本。他称 KV cache 是约束 coding agent 的"暴政"——它让路由不可能、阻止 subagent 共享上下文、迫使状态只能追加，像永远传递的全局变量。他估算 coding agent 平均带来 20-40% 的提速，而行业有斯德哥尔摩综合征，被动接受模型厂商给的任何东西而非协作推进真正需要的方向。关于 Anthropic IPO："'让他们治愈癌症'终将像玛丽·安托瓦内特的'让他们吃蛋糕'一样。"关于 ASI 安全：距离真正的自主性还有两个巨大突破，把 Bostrom 的《超级智能》当现实而非科幻读的人让他担忧。他的北极星："为什么我们能解决千禧年数学难题，却连会计都自动化不了？这应该让很多人觉得奇怪。"
