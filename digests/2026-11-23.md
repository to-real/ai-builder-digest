## No Priors — Powering AI-Driven Drug Discovery with President of Isomorphic Labs Max Jaderberg

*No Priors*

**Drug discovery costs have ballooned to $1.5B and 15 years per drug while approvals stay flat; Isomorphic Labs is betting that moving the entire process into computer simulation can break that curve.**

Max Jaderberg, President and founding team member of Isomorphic Labs (backed by Alphabet), came from DeepMind where he worked on AlphaGo and AlphaStar. The company was born in 2021 when AlphaFold proved that deep learning could generalize meaningfully in biology, the first real proof of concept that computational drug design was viable at scale.

The core problem Jaderberg identifies is structural: pharma treats each drug as a bespoke science project with no compounding learnings across projects. The empirical trial-and-error process is bounded by physics: how long a reaction takes, how long cells take to culture. Meanwhile, the low-hanging fruit has been picked, making each new discovery harder and more expensive.

Isomorphic's approach is to build AI simulation models that achieve "experimental accuracy," meaning you can trust computational predictions enough to skip most real-world iteration. Their internal leaderboards have a dotted line marking experimental accuracy as the target. When they do go into the real world, it's to validate, not to learn.

A striking data point: animal models (rats) are only about 30% predictive of human clinical toxicity. As Jaderberg puts it: "If one of my AI scientists came up to me and said, 'hey, we've got this amazing model, it's 30% predictive,' you're like, okay, maybe do a bit better than that." He sees opportunity in combining in vitro endpoints, organoids, and AI models to directly predict human toxicity, bypassing animal proxies entirely.

Isomorphic doesn't license its models. It uses them internally for its own drug pipeline (focused on oncology, immunology, and inflammation) and partners with pharma companies on hard problems they've been stuck on for decades. The models are disease-agnostic, target-class-agnostic, and modality-agnostic.

Jaderberg's five-year expectation: "I'd be really disappointed if we fast forward even five years' time, if the predictive understanding of disease biology has not step changed in a massive way."

药物研发成本已膨胀至每款药 15 亿美元、耗时 15 年，而新药批准数量却几乎停滞；Isomorphic Labs 押注于将整个流程搬入计算机模拟来打破这一困局。

Max Jaderberg 是 Isomorphic Labs（由 Alphabet 支持）的总裁及创始团队成员，此前在 DeepMind 参与 AlphaGo 和 AlphaStar 项目。公司成立于 2021 年，当时 AlphaFold 证明了深度学习能在生物学领域实现有意义的泛化，这是计算药物设计可大规模可行的第一个真正概念验证。

Jaderberg 指出的核心问题是结构性的：制药行业把每种药当作独立的科学项目，项目之间没有可复用的学习积累。经验性的试错流程受限于物理规律：反应需要多长时间、细胞培养需要多长时间。与此同时，容易发现的药物已被找完，每项新发现都更难、更贵。

Isomorphic 的方法是构建达到"实验精度"的 AI 模拟模型，意味着你可以信任计算预测，从而跳过大部分真实世界的迭代。他们内部排行榜上有一条虚线标记着实验精度目标。当他们进入真实世界时，是为了验证，而非学习。

一个惊人的数据点：动物模型（大鼠）对人类临床毒性的预测率仅约 30%。Jaderberg 说："如果我的一个 AI 科学家跑来跟我说，'我们有个很棒的模型，预测率 30%'，你会说，好吧，也许可以做得更好一点。"他认为可以结合体外实验终点、类器官和 AI 模型来直接预测人体毒性，完全绕过动物代理。

Isomorphic 不对外授权模型，而是用于内部药物管线（聚焦肿瘤学、免疫学和炎症），同时与制药公司合作解决他们困扰了数十年的难题。模型与疾病类型、靶点类别和分子模态无关。

Jaderberg 对五年的期望："如果五年后我们对疾病生物学的预测性理解没有发生质的飞跃，我会非常失望。"
