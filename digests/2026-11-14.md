## Guillermo Rauch

*CEO, Vercel*

**UI components are the x86 instruction set for agents — rigid framework abstractions are kryptonite for infinite creation machines.**

Rauch argues that in the agents era, high-level single-framework abstractions must be replaced by something deeper: packaged behaviors (UI SDK) that deliver accessibility, performance, and cohesion while imposing zero limitations on what UIs agents can generate. Agents are the new design engineers and need composable primitives, not rigid templates. UI SDK lets you have the cake and eat it too — saving tokens, errors, and effort without constraining generative possibility.

Rauch 认为，在 agent 时代，高层单一框架抽象必须被更深层的东西取代：打包的行为（UI SDK）提供无障碍、性能和一致性，同时对 agent 能生成的 UI 不设任何限制。Agent 是新的设计工程师，需要可组合的原语而非死板模板。UI SDK 让你鱼与熊掌兼得——省 token、省错误、省力气，却不束缚生成可能性。

## No Priors — Powering AI-Driven Drug Discovery with President of Isomorphic Labs Max Jaderberg

*No Priors*

**Drug discovery must move from the wet lab to the computer — the empirical process has found all the low-hanging fruit and can't reach further.**

Max Jaderberg, President and founding team member of Isomorphic Labs (backed by Alphabet, spun out of DeepMind in 2021), argues that the pharmaceutical industry's core problem is its empirically driven process. Drug approvals per year have stayed flat while costs ballooned from $100M to $1.5B and timelines stretched from 8 to 15 years. The low-hanging fruit found by trial-and-error screening is exhausted. Jaderberg's North Star: bring all drug discovery work onto a computer, achieving experimental accuracy in simulation models so you only enter the real world to validate, not to iterate. He points out that rat toxicity studies are only ~30% predictive of human clinical toxicity: "if one of my AI scientists came up to me and said, hey, we've got this amazing model, it's 30% predictive, you're like, okay, maybe do a bit better than that." Isomorphic builds its AI drug design engine in-house (general across disease areas, target classes, and modalities), generates proprietary data to feed scaling laws, and focuses internally on oncology and immunology/inflammation. They partner with pharma on hard problems teams have struggled with for 10-20 years. Jaderberg would be "really disappointed if we fast forward even five years' time, like, the predictive understanding of disease biology has not step changed in, like, a massive way."

Max Jaderberg，Isomorphic Labs 总裁及创始团队成员（Alphabet 支持，2021 年从 DeepMind 分拆），认为制药行业的核心问题是经验驱动流程。每年新药获批数持平，而成本从 1 亿美元膨胀到 15 亿美元，周期从 8 年拉长到 15 年。试错筛选能找到的低垂果实已耗尽。Jaderberg 的北极星：把所有药物发现工作搬到计算机上，让模拟模型达到实验精度，这样进入真实世界只为验证而非迭代。他指出大鼠毒性研究对人类临床毒性的预测率仅约 30%："如果我的 AI 科学家跑来说，嘿，我们有个超棒模型，预测率 30%，你会说，好吧，也许可以再好一点。"Isomorphic 自建 AI 药物设计引擎（跨疾病领域、靶点类别和分子模态通用），自产专有数据喂养 scaling law，内部聚焦肿瘤学和免疫/炎症。他们与药企合作攻克团队苦战 10-20 年的难题。Jaderberg 表示"如果五年后疾病生物学的预测性理解没有发生巨大跃迁，我会非常失望。"
