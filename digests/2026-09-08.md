## No Priors — Rethinking Legacy Data Infrastructure with Eon Co-Founders Ofir Ehrlich and Gonen Stein

*No Priors*

**Your data is your only moat in the AI era, but the rise of autonomous agents creates a security nightmare that legacy infrastructure cannot handle.**

Ofir Ehrlich and Gonen Stein, co-founders of Eon, explain why data has become the most critical asset for enterprises, citing Google's purchase of Spirit Airlines' data for $10 million as proof that datasets are the new gold. They argue that while cloud migration was a gradual shift, the AI era is happening on steroids, with non-technical employees building agents that handle sensitive data outside of organizational rules. This creates a new threat vector: legitimate AI agents with valid permissions that can compromise systems at extreme velocity. Legacy tools like ETL pipelines and data warehouses are too siloed for this agentic world; instead, companies need a unified data foundation that maps, classifies, and protects data while making it accessible for AI workflows. "It's a good thing and bad thing that everyone inside the organization can become builders," Stein notes, highlighting the chaos of democratized AI development.

在 AI 时代，数据是你唯一的护城河，但自主 Agent 的兴起制造了一个传统基础设施无法处理的安全噩梦。Eon 的联合创始人 Ofir Ehrlich 和 Gonen Stein 解释了为什么数据已成为企业最关键的资产，并以 Google 花 1000 万美元购买 Spirit Airlines 的数据为例，证明数据集是新的黄金。他们认为，虽然云迁移是一个渐进的过程，但 AI 时代正在“激素式”地爆发，非技术员工正在构建在组织规则之外处理敏感数据的 Agent。这创造了一个新的威胁向量：拥有合法权限的 AI Agent 可以以极快的速度破坏系统。传统的 ETL 管道和数据仓库等工具对于这个 Agent 世界来说过于孤立；相反，公司需要一个统一的数据基础，在为 AI 工作流提供数据访问的同时，对数据进行映射、分类和保护。“组织内的每个人都能成为开发者，这既是好事也是坏事，”Stein 指出，强调了 AI 开发民主化带来的混乱。
