## Anthropic Engineering

### Redesigning source control at Anthropic

**Anthropic replaced git with Scion, a from-scratch VCS, because AI agents pushed daily merges from 2,500 to 30,000 — and git wasn't built for that.**

Git assumes offline, decentralized human work. Anthropic's clients are almost all online agents. Scion flips the model: the server handles commits, merges, blame, and history; clients stay thin with bounded caches and sparse/virtualized checkouts. A changeset stores only what changed relative to its parent, content-addressed into blob storage — no packfiles, no locking, no refs to coordinate. Derived data structures (blame, path history, commit graph skip lists) are computed recursively and can be added or deferred independently.

The results: 200x smaller infrastructure footprint than the old git mirror fleet, checkouts two orders of magnitude faster, 25% CPU capacity savings in CI. A team of seven built Scion plus code review (Antique) and half a dozen related systems in five months while merge volume 10x'd. Claude wrote much of the code — including rewriting the ingestion pipeline to 20x less memory and 4x faster with no human guidance. Engineers shifted roles: "Very often, the role of the engineers was to tell Claude 'no.'" The team deployed "superfans" — Claude instances obsessively monitoring individual components, catching subtle issues traditional alerts miss.

Scion isn't a general git replacement, but Anthropic expects these scaling pressures to hit others within 6-12 months and is sharing their thinking so the ecosystem can adapt — particularly novel server architectures and comprehensive server-side RPCs that could extend git without breaking the model.

Git 假设的是离线、去中心化的人类工作方式，而 Anthropic 的客户端几乎全是在线 agent。Scion 翻转了模型：服务器处理提交、合并、blame 和历史；客户端只保留有界缓存和稀疏/虚拟化检出。changeset 只存储相对父节点的变更，内容寻址存入 blob storage——没有 packfile、没有锁、没有需要协调的 ref。派生数据结构（blame、路径历史、提交图跳表）递归计算，可独立添加或延迟。

结果：基础设施足迹比旧 git 镜像集群小 200 倍，检出速度快两个数量级，CI 节省 25% CPU 容量。七人团队在五个月内构建了 Scion、代码审查工具 Antique 及半打相关系统，同期合并量增长 10 倍。Claude 写了大量代码——包括在无人工指导下将 ingestion pipeline 重写为内存降低 20 倍、速度提升 4 倍。工程师的角色转变了："很多时候，工程师的角色就是对 Claude 说'不'。"团队还部署了"超级粉丝"——专注监控单个组件的 Claude 实例，能捕捉传统告警遗漏的细微问题。

Scion 不是通用的 git 替代品，但 Anthropic 预计这些扩展压力将在 6-12 个月内冲击其他公司，因此分享思路让生态适应——尤其是新型服务器架构和全面的服务端 RPC，可以在不破坏现有模型的情况下扩展 git。

## Training Data — David Silver and Lasse Espeholt of Ineffable Intelligence: A Superlearner for the Age of Experience

*Training Data*

**The path to superintelligence doesn't go through human data — it goes through systems that discover knowledge entirely on their own, from experience.**

David Silver (co-creator of AlphaGo/AlphaZero) and Lasse Espeholt (pioneer of scaled RL on GPUs) co-founded Ineffable Intelligence to build a "superlearner" — an agent with the depth to go from zero knowledge to beyond human understanding, and the breadth to learn in any environment. Their core conviction: LLMs are semi-autonomous, human-in-the-loop systems that can't reach true superintelligence because they're anchored to human data. Silver draws a direct analogy to AlphaGo vs AlphaZero: AlphaGo took two years with human data; AlphaZero, once they removed human priors, surpassed it in hours. The same principle at scale could unlock autonomous discovery of entirely new fields of knowledge.

Ineffable is building two things: the superlearner itself (a state-of-the-art RL stack on massive compute) and "crucibles" — simulated environments rich enough for emergent complexity to arise from simple reward functions. The key insight: you don't craft a reward that specifies "discover science." Simple objectives in sufficiently complex environments produce emergent complexity, just as evolution's simple fitness signal produced all of life's complexity. Their crucibles are Turing-complete worlds where agents can discover language, mathematics, economics, and technology from scratch.

On alignment, Silver argues language-based monitoring will fail as agents grow more powerful. He advocates building superintelligence first in simulation (faster, safer), developing a science of how superintelligences interact peacefully, and gating real-world deployment on that understanding. He predicts strong evidence within a year and true superintelligence within three.

David Silver（AlphaGo/AlphaZero 联合创造者）和 Lasse Espeholt（GPU 大规模 RL 先驱）共同创立 Ineffable Intelligence，要构建"超级学习者"——一个从零知识出发、深度能超越人类理解、广度能适应任何环境的 agent。他们的核心信念：LLM 是半自主、人在回路中的系统，因为锚定在人类数据上，无法达到真正的超级智能。Silver 直接类比 AlphaGo 与 AlphaZero：AlphaGo 花两年用人类数据；AlphaZero 去掉人类先验后，几小时就超越了它。同一原则放大到极致，可以解锁对全新知识领域的自主发现。

Ineffable 在构建两样东西：超级学习者本身（大规模算力上的前沿 RL 技术栈）和"坩埚"——足够丰富的模拟环境，让简单奖励函数就能催生涌现复杂性。关键洞察：你不需要精心设计"发现科学"的奖励。简单目标在足够复杂的环境中会产生涌现复杂性，就像进化的简单适应度信号产生了生命的全部复杂性。他们的坩埚是图灵完备的世界，agent 可以从零发现语言、数学、经济学和技术。

关于对齐，Silver 认为基于语言的监控在 agent 更强大时会失效。他主张先在模拟中构建超级智能（更快、更安全），发展超级智能如何和平交互的科学，并以此作为现实世界部署的门槛。他预言一年内出现强证据，三年内实现真正的超级智能。

> "Once we got AlphaZero right, we gave up on the human data... it took just a few hours to outperform the two years of work which we'd done with human in the loop."

> "一旦我们把 AlphaZero 做对了，就放弃了人类数据……仅仅几个小时就超越了我们在人在回路中花两年做出的成果。"
