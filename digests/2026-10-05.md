## Ryo Lu

*Designer, Cursor*

**AI makes creation frictionless, which risks converging everything to the mean — unless we approach making things like artists.**

Ryo Lu argues that when everyone looks at everyone else to decide what to make, we stop making anything of our own. AI supercharges this loop: it can draw from more references than we could see in a lifetime, but "access to every perspective isn't the same as having one." The result is "a tidal wave of mid." Software, like art, should express beliefs about how life should feel — but we rarely talk about software as a medium, only as an industry. The core question: "What is the point of everyone being able to make something, if we all end up making the same thing?"

Ryo Lu 认为，当每个人都看别人来决定做什么，我们就不再创造属于自己的东西。AI 让这个循环更无摩擦：它能调用的参考比我们一辈子看到的还多，但"拥有所有视角的访问权不等于拥有一个视角"。结果是"平庸的潮汐"。软件像艺术一样，应该表达关于生活应有感受的信念——但我们很少把软件当作媒介，只当作产业。核心问题："如果人人都能创造，但最终都做出一样的东西，那有什么意义？"

## The MAD Podcast — Who Feeds the GPUs? Inside AI's Hidden $30B Layer

*The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck*

**The software infrastructure layer between hardware and models is the operating system for AI — and it's where the value accrues.**

VAST Data CEO Renen Hallak builds the middle layer of Jensen Huang's "five-layer AI cake": the software infrastructure that feeds data to GPUs at scale. His architecture, "disaggregated shared everything," flips the old shared-nothing paradigm — all nodes see all information as if locally attached, enabling single clusters of multiple exabytes delivering tens of terabytes per second. The demand shock is real: one AI cloud customer planned for 500 petabytes over three years, then came back a quarter later needing an extra 2 exabytes on top. Hallak's most provocative argument: every organization's IP will eventually be distilled into model weights through agent-driven fine-tuning, creating a marketplace where you can lease a "carpenter robot brain module" for a week without exposing the weights. VAST's new "Data Enclave" uses Nvidia's encrypted memory to let enterprises run inference on-prem — the model builder's weights stay encrypted, the enterprise's data stays private, and neither side sees the other's IP. On the macro question of whether demand will sustain: "We had a customer say we're probably going to need about 500 petabytes over the next three years. Last week, came back and said, we're gonna need an extra two exabytes on top of that." On where value accrues: historically, the software infrastructure layer produces the most valuable companies (Microsoft, Amazon, Apple, Google) — and there's no reason this revolution differs.

VAST Data CEO Renen Hallak 构建的是 Jensen Huang "五层 AI 蛋糕"的中间层：大规模向 GPU 喂数据的软件基础设施。他的"分解式共享一切"架构颠覆了旧的 shared-nothing 范式——所有节点都能像本地访问一样看到所有信息，实现多 EB 单集群、数十 TB/秒吞吐。需求冲击是真实的：一个 AI 云客户原计划三年用 500 PB，一个季度后回来说还需要额外 2 EB。Hallak 最有冲击力的论点：每个组织的 IP 最终会通过 agent 驱动的 fine-tuning 蒸馏成模型权重，形成你可以按周租用"木匠机器人脑模块"而不暴露权重的市场。VAST 新的"Data Enclave"用 Nvidia 加密内存让企业在本地跑推理——模型方的权重加密，企业的数据私密，双方都看不到对方的 IP。关于需求是否可持续："有客户说三年大概需要 500 PB，上周回来说还需要额外 2 EB。"关于价值归属：历史上软件基础设施层产生了最有价值的公司（Microsoft、Amazon、Apple、Google）——这场革命没有理由不同。
