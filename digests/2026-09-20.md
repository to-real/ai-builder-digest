## No Priors — Why Diffusion Will Win AI Inference with Inception Co-Founder and CEO Stefano Ermon

*No Priors*

**Diffusion models will win the inference battle because they parallelize workloads on GPUs, unlike sequential autoregressive models.**

扩散模型将赢得推理之战，因为它们可以在 GPU 上并行化工作负载，而不像自回归模型那样是顺序执行的。

Stefano Ermon, Stanford professor and CEO of Inception, argues that while autoregressive models won the training race due to parallelization, they remain sequential at inference, making them inefficient on GPUs. Inception's Mercury models match frontier speed models (like GPT-4o mini) in quality but are significantly faster, enabling voice agents to run on standard NVIDIA GPUs instead of custom chips like Cerebras. Ermon bets on "inference time scaling" and intelligence per dollar, stating, "The bitter lesson is that the more parallel solution is the one that is eventually going to win."

斯坦福大学教授、Inception CEO Stefano Ermon 认为，虽然自回归模型因并行化赢得了训练竞赛，但它们在推理时仍然是顺序的，这使得它们在 GPU 上效率低下。Inception 的 Mercury 模型在质量上与前沿速度模型（如 GPT-4o mini）相当，但速度显著更快，使语音 agent 能够在标准的 NVIDIA GPU 上运行，而无需 Cerebras 等定制芯片。Ermon 押注于“推理时算力扩展”和每美元智能，他说：“苦涩的教训是，最终获胜的将是更并行的解决方案。”
