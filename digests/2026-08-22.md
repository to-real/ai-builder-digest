## Anthropic Engineering: Scaling Managed Agents: Decoupling the brain from the hands

**Managed Agents virtualizes the session, harness, and sandbox to create a durable, stateless architecture that outlasts specific model implementations.**

By decoupling the "brain" (Claude and harness) from the "hands" (sandboxes) and the "session" (event log), Anthropic solved the "pets vs cattle" problem where container failures meant lost sessions. This architecture reduces time-to-first-token by 60% and allows the harness to be rebooted from the durable session log without losing state, while keeping credentials secure outside the sandbox.

通过将“大脑”（Claude 和 harness）与“手”（沙箱）及“会话”（事件日志）解耦，Anthropic 解决了“宠物与牲畜”问题，即容器故障意味着会话丢失。这种架构将首字节延迟（TTFT）降低了 60%，并允许 harness 从持久化的会话日志中重启而不丢失状态，同时将凭证安全地保存在沙箱之外。

*No Priors*

**The brain is literally a computer, and treating it as one allows us to restore senses like vision in ways traditional medicine cannot.**

Max Hodak, CEO of Science (formerly Neuralink), argues that the brain is a computer that processes inputs through a small number of "wires" like the optic nerve. Hodak believes this "device view" of medicine—treating the brain as hardware to be interfaced with rather than biology to be drugged—will lead to more reliable outcomes than the "random walk" of small molecule discovery. He also discusses the "platonic representation hypothesis," noting that the geometry of concepts in AI models closely mirrors that in biological brains, suggesting both are grabbing onto the same underlying data manifold.

Science 公司 CEO（前 Neuralink）Max Hodak 认为，大脑是一台通过视神经等少量“线路”处理输入的计算机。他公司的 Prima 视网膜假体刚刚获得欧洲监管批准，通过向植入视网膜下方的芯片投射图像，让盲人恢复形态视觉。Hodak 相信这种医学的“设备观”——将大脑视为需要连接的硬件而非需要用药的生物学——将比小分子发现的“随机游走”带来更可靠的结果。他还讨论了“柏拉图表征假说”，指出 AI 模型中概念的几何结构与生物大脑中的非常相似，这表明两者都抓住了相同的底层数据流形。
