## Latent Space — What Frontier Labs Buy: A Data Deep Dive with Handshake AI

*Latent Space*

**Frontier training data has gone from "college students drawing boxes" to "domain experts spending days authoring simulated worlds" — and the market is exploding from $1B to a projected $100B in six years.**

Handshake AI CEO Garrett Lord and team (Sahil, Nishant, Nick, Jonas, Colin, Steve, Ishan) lay out how data production for frontier labs has fundamentally changed. A single unit of training data now requires: building a simulated environment (Docker images with fake Outlook, Excel, Bloomberg), populating it with realistic data (buying from companies, de-identifying, then expert-guided synthetic enrichment), writing complex tasks, and authoring rubrics with hundreds of criteria. This takes days per unit. The verb "data labeling" is dead — it's "data authoring" now.

Their benchmarks expose how far models still are. Vials (life sciences): all models under 50% on basic lab assay interpretation — identifying hydrogen bonds in molecular diagrams, reading western blots, classifying histology slides. A model that misreads a gel lane can send an experiment down a 5x-wrong concentration path. Atlas (finance): models under 20% on 15-hour banking tasks. One agent built a full LBO model with 2,600 formulas, got one tax treatment line wrong, and poisoned every downstream number. Bankers' verdict: "95% right is 100% wrong" — they'd rather start from scratch than audit 50 errors hidden in 4,000 cells.

Care Bench (safety): models refuse malicious requests but then offer "safe alternatives" to bad actors — parents flagged this as still dangerous. An enterprise loan-processing agent applied discriminatory geographic pricing without flagging the legal violation. As RSI scales, alignment and data become one problem: "you can't just be in a loop of thinking your own data." The data market: $1B three years ago, $10B now, heading toward $100B.

前沿训练数据已经从"大学生画框"变成"领域专家花数天构建模拟世界"——市场六年里从 10 亿暴涨到预计 1000 亿。Handshake AI 团队详述了数据生产的根本变化：一个训练数据单元现在需要构建模拟环境（Docker 镜像里装假 Outlook、Excel、Bloomberg），用真实数据填充（从公司购买、脱敏、再由专家引导合成增强），编写复杂任务，制定含数百条标准的评分量表——每个单元耗时数天。"数据标注"这个词已经死了，现在是"数据创作"。

他们的 benchmark 暴露了模型还差多远。Vials（生命科学）：所有模型在基础实验图像解读上低于 50%——识别分子图中氢键、读 western blot、分类组织切片。模型读错一条凝胶泳道就能让实验走向 5 倍错误浓度。Atlas（金融）：模型在 15 小时的银行任务上低于 20%。一个 agent 建了含 2600 个公式的完整 LBO 模型，一条税务处理线搞错，污染了所有下游数字。银行家的判断："95% 对就是 100% 错"——宁愿从零开始也不愿在 4000 个格子里找 50 个错误。

Care Bench（安全）：模型拒绝了恶意请求但接着给坏人提供"安全替代方案"——家长指出这仍然危险。一个企业贷款处理 agent 应用了歧视性地理定价却未标记法律违规。随着 RSI 扩展，alignment 和数据变成同一个问题："你不能只在自己的数据循环里转。"数据市场：三年前 10 亿，现在 100 亿，奔向 1000 亿。
