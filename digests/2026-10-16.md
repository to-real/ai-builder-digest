## AI & I by Every — How Hugging Face's Investor Spots Winners

*AI & I by Every*

**Product insight generation is exponentially more valuable when AI compresses iteration cycles from months to days.**

Matt Hartman, founder and managing partner at Factorial Capital, wrote the first check into Hugging Face while at Betaworks. His investment philosophy: find the "newly technically possible" and "newly programmable." Hugging Face itself started as a consumer chatbot for teenagers with poor retention — the team's honesty about those metrics, their decision to open-source Transformers, and their flexibility to expand beyond language models into robotics, bio, and weather models were the three pivotal decisions. Hartman backs technical founders because their pivot range is wider: "the companies that have been most successful that I've been involved with are ones where the pivot was about the technology, not about necessarily market understanding of a particular vertical." Factorial Capital's structure is a solo GP with venture partners drawn from the portfolio — Clem from Hugging Face introduced Nomic, Nomic introduced Patronus, creating a "family tree" of technical founders. On what matters now: "if it used to take two months to get the insight and code it and get it back in front of the user, it might take two days now instead. The ability to generate product insights has got to be exponentially more valuable." He also built Ghost Pepper, a local voice-to-text tool that hit #1 on Hacker News, and learned that vibe-coded tools still need maintenance — giving him new appreciation for SaaS products that continuously iterate. His current theses: infrastructure/devtools, frontier non-language models (robotics, science), and application-layer companies where founders go full-stack down to model-level optimizations.

Matt Hartman 是 Factorial Capital 创始人兼管理合伙人，在 Betaworks 期间给 Hugging Face 开了第一张支票。他的投资哲学：寻找"新近技术可行的"和"新近可编程的"。Hugging Face 最初是面向青少年的聊天机器人，留存很差——团队对指标的诚实、开源 Transformers 的决定、以及从语言模型扩展到机器人/生物/天气模型的灵活性，是三个关键决策。Hartman 偏好技术创始人，因为他们的 pivot 范围更广："我参与的最成功的公司，pivot 都是围绕技术，而非某个垂直行业的市场认知。" Factorial 的结构是 solo GP 加来自投资组合的 venture partner——Hugging Face 的 Clem 介绍 Nomic，Nomic 介绍 Patronus，形成技术创始人的"家族树"。关于当下什么最重要："如果以前从获得洞察到编码到回到用户面前要两个月，现在可能只要两天。生成产品洞察的能力价值必然呈指数级增长。"他还做了 Ghost Pepper，一个本地语音转文字工具，登上 HN 第一，但发现 vibe code 的工具仍需维护——让他重新欣赏持续迭代的 SaaS 产品。当前投资主题：基础设施/开发者工具、前沿非语言模型（机器人、科学）、以及创始人能全栈下探到模型层优化的应用层公司。
