## The MAD Podcast — When AI Improves Itself | Richard Socher (Recursive)

*The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck*

**AI that recursively improves itself will unlock scientific discovery at a scale humans can't reach — but the path runs through simulation, not magic.**

Richard Socher, one of the most cited AI researchers and now CEO of Recursive ($650M raised), argues that scientific progress has stalled because knowledge evolved from a body into a labyrinth of 34,000 journals — no human can be a Renaissance mind anymore. AI will do for biology what calculus did for physics: weave together impossibly complex systems. His core mechanism: "Anything you can simulate, AI will solve." That covers games, math, and programming today; biology and medicine next, once we build virtual cells and collect enough perturbation data. Hallucination, usually treated as a bug, becomes a feature in science — it's how you explore novel proteins and molecules outside the training distribution. His "Eureka Machine" rests on four pillars: (1) LMs ingesting all human knowledge, (2) scientific measurements beyond human perception, (3) simulation environments, and (4) real-world robotic experimentation. Recursive's strategy is AI-for-AI first — achieve the equivalent of 50,000 PhDs in AI research capability — then turn that intelligence toward natural sciences. Compute is the binding constraint; Recursive committed $410M of its $670M raise to a single Amazon compute deal. Socher is skeptical of hard takeoff: biology demands real-world trials that can't be simulated away, but drug timelines are already compressing from 8 years to 6-18 months for multiple candidates. As he puts it: "Boy, are we far away from the true upper bounds of any of the spaces of intelligence."

Richard Socher 是被引用最多的 AI 研究者之一，现任 Recursive CEO（融资 6.5 亿美元）。他认为科学进步已经停滞，因为知识从一体变成了 34,000 本期刊的迷宫——再也没人能成为文艺复兴式全才。AI 对生物学的意义如同微积分对物理学：把不可能手动整合的复杂系统编织起来。核心机制："任何你能模拟的东西，AI 都能解决。"今天这覆盖了游戏、数学和编程；下一步是生物和医学——一旦建出虚拟细胞并收集足够的扰动数据。幻觉通常被视为缺陷，在科学中却是特性——它是探索训练分布之外新蛋白质和分子的方式。"Eureka Machine"基于四根支柱：(1) 摄取全部人类知识的 LLM，(2) 超越人类感知的科学测量，(3) 模拟环境，(4) 真实世界的机器人实验。Recursive 的策略是先做 AI-for-AI——达到相当于 50,000 个 AI 研究 PhD 的能力——再将智能转向自然科学。算力是硬约束；Recursive 把 6.7 亿美元融资中的 4.1 亿投入了与 Amazon 的单一算力交易。Socher 不相信硬起飞：生物学需要无法被模拟替代的真实试验，但药物周期已从 8 年压缩到 6-18 个月出多个候选。正如他所说："我们在任何智能空间的真正上限面前，还差得远呢。"
