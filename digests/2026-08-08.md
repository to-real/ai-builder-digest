## The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck — How to Build Long-Horizon AI Agents — Mitch Troyanovsky, Basis

*The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck*

**Building reliable long-horizon agents requires "behavior specs" and process supervision rather than just outcome-based rewards, as the latter doesn't generalize to real-world production.**

Mitch Troyanovsky, co-founder of Basis, explains that while LLMs have large working memories, they lack inherent long-term memory, making coherence over hours or days the fundamental challenge for agents. He argues that relying solely on outcome-based evals is insufficient because passing 100 tests doesn't guarantee real-world reliability; instead, Basis uses "behavior specs"—markdown files defining how agents should act (e.g., citing primary sources)—to enforce good process. Troyanovsky also emphasizes that context is effectively "runtime training data," meaning the quality of an agent's documentation and ontology is as critical as the model itself.

Basis 联合创始人 Mitch Troyanovsky 解释说，虽然 LLM 拥有巨大的工作记忆，但缺乏固有的长期记忆，这使得在数小时或数天内的连贯性成为 Agent 面临的根本挑战。他认为仅依赖基于结果的评估是不够的，因为通过 100 次测试并不能保证现实世界的可靠性；相反，Basis 使用“行为规范”——即定义 Agent 应如何行动的 markdown 文件（例如引用主要来源）——来强制执行良好的流程。Troyanovsky 还强调，上下文实际上是“运行时训练数据”，这意味着 Agent 的文档和本体的质量与模型本身同样重要。
