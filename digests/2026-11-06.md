## AI & I by Every — The Inside Story of the 50 Who Built Modern AI With Kevin Roose

*AI & I by Every*

**The most important artifact of the AI race is Dario Amodei's 2017 "Big Blob of Compute" memo — it convinced OpenAI to abandon narrow models and bet on scale.**

Kevin Roose, New York Times bestselling author and host of Machine Gods, spent a year interviewing 150+ people to document how modern AI was built. The book's most revealing find: Dario Amodei's internally famous 2017 memo at OpenAI arguing against narrow, task-specific models and for "assembling large unstructured masses of computational capacity" pointed at general problems. As Roose puts it, "you see in almost real time, like the light bulb going off in Dario's head where he's like, oh, we should just scale these models." Roose also discovered that AI leaders — Dario, Demis, Sam — privately want to slow the frontier to buy safety teams time, and he believes federal regulation won't arrive without a crisis. He wrote the book using a "Council of Claudes" — a swarm of agents with distinct personas (skeptic, historian, craftsperson) that caught factual errors his human editors missed. His framing for AI usage: "weightlifting" tasks where struggle is the benefit (parenting), versus "forklifting" tasks where you just need to move something from A to B (legal paperwork).

Kevin Roose 是《纽约时报》畅销书作者、Machine Gods 主持人，花了一年采访 150 多人，记录现代 AI 是如何建成的。书中最有冲击力的发现：Dario Amodei 2017 年在 OpenAI 写的那份内部著名备忘录，反对窄任务模型，主张"组装大型非结构化计算块"来解通用问题。Roose 说："你几乎实时看到 Dario 脑中灯泡亮起——我们就该 scale 这些模型。"他还发现 AI 领袖们——Dario、Demis、Sam——私下希望放慢前沿节奏，给安全团队争取时间；联邦监管不会在没有危机的情况下到来。他写书时用了"Council of Claudes"——一组有不同人设的 agent（怀疑者、历史学家、匠人），抓到了人类编辑没发现的 factual error。他对 AI 使用的框架："举重"任务——挣扎本身就是收益（比如育儿）；"叉车"任务——只需要把东西从 A 搬到 B（比如法律文书）。
