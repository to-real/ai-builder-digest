## Anthropic Engineering: How we contain Claude across products

**The deterministic boundary is what gets hit when everything probabilistic misses.**

Anthropic details the security architecture behind their agentic products (claude.ai, Claude Code, Claude Cowork), focusing on "containment" as the primary defense. They describe three isolation patterns: ephemeral containers for web, human-in-the-loop sandboxes for developers, and local VMs for general knowledge workers. The post reveals critical security lessons, such as the risk of "user as an injection vector" where a phished employee can bypass model safeguards, and exfiltration risks through "approved domains" like their own API. They argue that while model alignment is crucial, environmental defenses—sandboxes, egress controls, and filesystem boundaries—are the hard limits that prevent catastrophic failures when probabilistic defenses fail.

Anthropic 详细介绍了其 agentic 产品（claude.ai、Claude Code、Claude Cowork）背后的安全架构，将“遏制”作为主要防御手段。他们描述了三种隔离模式：用于网页的临时容器、面向开发者的人机协同沙箱，以及面向普通知识工作者的本地 VM。文章揭示了关键的安全教训，例如“用户作为注入向量”的风险，即被钓鱼的员工可以绕过模型安全措施，以及通过“批准的域名”（如他们自己的 API）进行数据泄露的风险。他们认为，虽然模型对齐至关重要，但当概率性防御失败时，环境防御——沙箱、出口控制和文件系统边界——是防止灾难性失败的硬性限制。

## No Priors — Building an Autonomous Enterprise for Real-World Services with Netic Founder Melisa Tokmak

*No Priors*

**Robotics is far off for essential services because the physical world is too unstandardized; the immediate opportunity is autonomous orchestration.**

Melisa Tokmak, founder and CEO of Netic, discusses building AI that manages the operations of essential service businesses like HVAC, plumbing, and pet care. She explains that Netic acts as an autonomous layer between the company and the customer, handling everything from understanding needs to dispatching labor. Tokmak chose to build this infrastructure layer rather than a roll-up because she wanted a product that could scale across any business, not just the ones she acquired. She argues that robotics is still decades away for these industries because buildings and homes lack the standardization required for robots to navigate effectively. Instead, she focuses on "autonomous enterprises" where AI handles all cognitive tasks so humans can focus purely on the physical labor and service quality. She also shares her hiring philosophy, looking for candidates with a history of long-term agency and commitment rather than those seeking quick exits or chasing "shiny objects."

Netic 创始人兼 CEO Melisa Tokmak 讨论了构建用于管理暖通空调、管道和宠物护理等基本服务业务运营的 AI。她解释说，Netic 充当公司与客户之间的自主层，处理从理解需求到调度人工的所有事务。Tokmak 选择构建这一基础设施层而不是进行整合，因为她想要一个可以跨任何业务扩展的产品，而不仅仅是她收购的业务。她认为，对于这些行业来说，机器人技术仍然有几十年的路要走，因为建筑物和家庭缺乏机器人有效导航所需的标准化。相反，她专注于“自主企业”，即 AI 处理所有认知任务，以便人类可以纯粹专注于体力劳动和服务质量。她还分享了她的招聘理念，寻找具有长期主动性和承诺历史的候选人，而不是那些寻求快速退出或追逐“光鲜事物”的人。
