## Thibault Sottiaux

*Codex & ChatGPT, OpenAI*

**Thibault Sottiaux: Comparing AI prices by token count is a misleading trap; focus on cost per outcome instead.**

An OpenAI token is not equivalent to a token in another model; for example, GPT-5.6 Sol uses about 34.5% fewer tokens than Claude Opus 5 to produce the exact same text. Just as a pizza cut into more slices doesn't cost less, a lower price per token can actually result in a higher bill, making "price per successful outcome" the only metric that truly matters.

OpenAI 的 token 与其他模型的 token 并不等价；例如，为了生成完全相同的文本，GPT-5.6 Sol 使用的 token 数量比 Claude Opus 5 少约 34.5%。就像把披萨切成更多块并不会降低总成本一样，较低的每 token 价格实际上可能导致更高的账单，因此“每次成功结果的成本”才是唯一真正重要的指标。

## AI & I by Every — Why the Next Hit AI Product Will Be Social (Best of the Pod)

*AI & I by Every*

**The next massive AI consumer product will likely be social and multiplayer, built by product geniuses rather than deep technical founders.**

The next wave of AI winners will be defined by product intuition and social dynamics, not raw technical capability. Sarah Tavel, a Partner at Benchmark who was early at Pinterest, traces a historical shift from the technical dominance of Google's founders to the product-centric success of Instagram and Snapchat. She argues that while ChatGPT represents the "technical founder" era—similar to Google's text box—the infrastructure is maturing enough for "product geniuses" to build social experiences where users learn from each other. "What's the multiplayer network effect type experience? Someone's gonna create a UGC type community where there are people who are really, really good, make it so much easier for the rest of us." She predicts a bifurcation between "worky" tools like ChatGPT and personal AI platforms, emphasizing that trust and status-seeking incentives will drive the next generation of AI adoption.

下一波 AI 的赢家将由产品直觉和社交动态定义，而非原始技术能力。Benchmark 合伙人、Pinterest 早期员工 Sarah Tavel 追溯了从 Google 创始人的技术主导地位到 Instagram 和 Snapchat 产品成功的历史转变。她认为，虽然 ChatGPT 代表了“技术创始人”时代——类似于 Google 的文本框——但基础设施正在成熟，足以让“产品天才”构建用户可以相互学习的社交体验。“多人互动的网络效应体验是什么？会有人创建一个 UGC 类型的社区，那里有非常擅长的人，让我们其他人更容易使用。”她预测“工作类”工具（如 ChatGPT）和个人 AI 平台将出现分化，并强调信任和地位追求的激励将推动下一代 AI 的采用。
