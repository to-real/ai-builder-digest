## Andrej Karpathy

*AI Researcher, Former Director of AI at Tesla*

**Karpathy built HackerTube by letting Claude Code do everything, including deploying to a VPS and hardening the server, in roughly 2 hours.**

He filtered 43M Hacker News comments down to 6,454 YouTube videos, creating a recommendation engine that beats YouTube's own. The only manual work was creating a Google Cloud API key, booting a VPS, and buying a domain. Claude Code (Opus 5.5, Medium effort) handled the rest: update, secure, harden, deploy, optimize. His takeaway: "Many awesome projects that would have normally taken weeks/months even can now be few-shotted for breakfast."

他用 Claude Code 把 4300 万条 Hacker News 评论筛到 6454 个 YouTube 视频，做出了一个推荐体验远超 YouTube 本身的播放器。手动操作只有三步：创建 API key、启动 VPS、买域名，其余全交给 Claude Code（Opus 5.5 Medium effort）完成。他的核心观察：原本需要数周甚至数月的项目，现在早餐时间就能 few-shot 搞定。

## Aaron Levie

*CEO, Box*

**Levie: we're moving from agents helping us do work, to us helping agents do work.**

Everyone in information-heavy roles (coding, legal, cybersecurity, biotech) is becoming a manager of agent teams rather than an IC with AI leverage. The critical shift: your output is now decoupled from hours worked, because agents parallelize execution. Organizations that orient around this first will see compounding gains.

信息密集型岗位（编程、法律、安全、生物科技）的人正在从"用 AI 加速的个体贡献者"变成"agent 团队的管理者"。关键转变：产出与工作时长脱钩，因为 agent 可以并行执行。率先围绕这一逻辑重组运营的组织将获得复利优势。

## Unsupervised Learning — Ep 95: Modal CEO on the Future of Inference, the Chaos of the GPU Market & Origins of the Sandbox Bet

*Unsupervised Learning (Redpoint AI)*

**The GPU market is still an "open air drug market" and durable differentiation in inference comes from being a multi-product platform, not from serving tokens faster.**

Modal CEO Erik Bernhardsson has spent five years building compute infrastructure from scratch, including a custom file system, container runtime, and scheduler, deliberately avoiding Kubernetes and Docker. That bet now pays off in sub-second cold starts and GPU snapshotting via RDMA peer-to-peer. Modal works with 25 clouds and does 2-3 compute deals per week, yet the market remains wildly inefficient. His GTC experience: "It was like an open air drug market, but GPUs. Walking around, talking to people, trying to get GPUs, meeting people in hotel lobbies, exchanging numbers." Compute isn't fungible enough for an exchange because reliability, networking, and region vary enormously.

Sandboxes, now a $300M run-rate business (one-third of Modal's revenue), started as an asymmetric bet in 2023. A prototype took a few days; it exploded when models got good at writing code. LLM inference, also one-third of revenue, was triggered by the DeepSeek moment. On differentiation, Erik is blunt: raw token serving is a race to the bottom commoditized by OpenRouter. The durable moat is the full platform: sandboxes, reinforcement learning, fine-tuning, batch processing. "Customers don't wanna buy single trick ponies."

On agents and engineering: senior engineers adopt coding agents more than juniors because "it's like giving them 10 interns." Deprecation is now harder because agents are trained on old code in the wild. And after reading Anthropic's S-1, Erik flipped his view on GPU prices: Anthropic is profitable enough to keep buying capacity aggressively, so prices will likely rise over the next 12 months.

Modal CEO Erik Bernhardsson 五年来从零构建计算基础设施，自研文件系统、容器运行时和调度器，刻意不用 Kubernetes 和 Docker。这个赌注现在兑现为亚秒级冷启动和基于 RDMA P2P 的 GPU 快照。Modal 对接 25 家云、每周做 2-3 笔算力交易，但市场仍极度低效。他在 GTC 的体验："就像露天毒品市场，不过是 GPU。到处走动找人拿 GPU，在酒店大堂交换号码。"算力不够标准化到能上交易所，因为可靠性、网络和地域差异太大。

Sandboxes 现在是 3 亿美元年化收入（Modal 收入的三分之一），2023 年起步时只是个不对称赌注，原型几天就写完，等模型写代码能力上来后爆发。LLM inference 也是收入的三分之一，由 DeepSeek 时刻触发。关于差异化，Erik 很直白：纯卖 token 是逐底竞争，OpenRouter 已经在商品化底层。持久护城河是全平台：sandbox、RL、fine-tuning、批处理。"客户不想买单功能产品。"

关于 agent 和工程：资深工程师比初级更积极采用 coding agent，因为"就像给了他们 10 个实习生"。废弃功能变难了，因为 agent 的训练数据里有旧代码。读完 Anthropic S-1 后，Erik 翻转了对 GPU 价格的判断：Anthropic 足够盈利会持续激进采购，未来 12 个月价格大概率上涨。
