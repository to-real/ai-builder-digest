**Replit's agent swarm research: decentralized swarms scale better than centralized ones, and individual model intelligence poorly predicts collective performance.**

Masad shared new Replit research on scaling laws for agent swarms. Key findings: Opus 5.5 went from 3.7x speedup with one agent to 18.9x with 32 agents on NumPy within a fixed two-hour window. GPT-6.2 Astra was the strongest solo agent but showed negative scaling beyond 8 agents. Models spontaneously developed organizational structures, with decentralized swarms outperforming centralized coordination. The parallelizability of the problem strongly predicted swarm performance. Replit is open-sourcing Optimization-Bench for further research.

Masad 分享了 Replit 关于 agent swarm scaling law 的新研究。关键发现：在固定两小时窗口内，Opus 5.5 在 NumPy 上从单 agent 的 3.7 倍加速提升到 32 个 agent 的 18.9 倍。GPT-6.2 Astra 是最强的单 agent，但超过 8 个 agent 后出现负向 scaling。模型自发形成组织结构，去中心化 swarm 优于中心化协调。问题的可并行性是 swarm 表现的强预测因子。Replit 同时开源了 Optimization-Bench 以推动这一研究方向。

## Aaron Levie

*CEO, Box*

**Agents decouple services business output from headcount, letting a single engineer produce the output of dozens, but human experts remain essential on the front end to ensure correct outcomes.**

Levie argues that services businesses were always constrained by headcount: no matter the demand, there was finite talent, and customers always wanted work done faster, cheaper, and better. Agents change this calculus entirely: "A single engineer can have the output of dozens of engineers before. A single designer can probably build out your entire design system and brand." But Levie cautions this isn't all-agents: "The key is to still have experts on the front end that actually can ensure the right outcomes are delivered from agents." He predicts this transformation will hit cybersecurity, advertising, legal, accounting, and IT transformation, calling it a great time to start or transform one of these businesses.

Levie 认为服务业一直受人头数限制：无论需求多大，人才是有限的，而客户总是希望更快、更便宜、更好。Agent 彻底改变了这个计算："一个工程师可以产出以前几十个工程师的量。一个设计师可能就能搭建你整个设计系统和品牌。"但 Levie 警告这不是全用 agent："关键是在前端仍然要有专家确保 agent 交付的结果是正确的。"他预测这一变革将冲击网络安全、广告、法律、会计和 IT 转型领域，认为现在是创办或转型这类企业的好时机。
