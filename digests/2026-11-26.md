## Amjad Masad

*CEO, Replit*

**America can build the best frontier models and still lose the global AI race — open models are the only way to win adoption.**

Masad argues that governments worldwide want sovereign AI they can run on their own soil. If American companies won't meet that demand, Chinese companies will, and once a country builds around Chinese models, it adopts Chinese infrastructure and chips too. He draws the Huawei analogy: supplying telecom infrastructure creates lasting strategic influence that's expensive to disrupt. Jensen's backing of the American open-model ecosystem matters because it gives startups freedom to compete and gives countries a reason to build on American technology. Separately, Masad notes that token demand grows in step functions: auto-complete to chat to agent to long-running autonomous agents to swarms. Each harness innovation unlocks an order of magnitude more token consumption, with a lag before users figure out what to do with new tools.

Masad 认为全球各国政府都想要主权 AI，即能在自己的土壤和基础设施上运行的模型。如果美国公司不满足这个需求，中国公司就会填补，而一旦某个国家围绕中国模型构建，就会同时采用中国的基础设施和芯片。他引用华为的先例：供应电信基础设施会创造持久的战略影响力，替换成本极高。Jensen 对美国开源模型生态的支持至关重要，因为它既给创业公司竞争的自由，也给各国一个基于美国技术构建的理由。此外，Masad 指出 token 需求以阶梯式增长：auto-complete → chat → agent → 长期自主 agent → swarm。每一次 harness 创新都释放一个数量级的 token 消耗潜力，但用户搞清楚如何使用新工具之间总有一段滞后期。

## Aaron Levie

*CEO, Box*

**AI is creating 10X productivity deltas between teams within the same company, a fundamentally different adoption pattern from any previous technology.**

Levie observes that previous tech adoption (cloud, digital transformation) happened at the firm level with relatively standardized uplift across teams. ## Garry Tan

*President & CEO, Y Combinator*

**Models have outpaced what many customers need, and society has hit its absorption limit, but a step function in agency and taste is still available.**

Tan identifies two brakes on AI progress. First, the models have reached beyond what some customers even need, though he suspects there's still a step function in agency and taste available even if models can do the tasks today. He also declared "YC is the YC for hard tech."

Tan 指出 AI 进展的两个刹车。第一，模型已经超出了部分客户的实际需求，但他认为即使在模型已经能完成任务的今天，agency 和 taste 方面仍有阶梯式提升的空间。第二，社会吸收技术的能力已经触顶：人们不喜欢自己的工作方式被打乱，这给知识工作流程的变革速度设下了天然上限。他还宣称"YC 就是 hard tech 领域的 YC"。

**Jensen Huang's return to NVIDIA doesn't undo the geopolitical damage: the chip smuggling into China was real, the export control rule is now law, and the political backlash from both parties is just beginning.**

Jordan Schneider，China Talk 主持人、追踪中美关系十年的观察者，以长期关注半导体出口管制的精准视角拆解了 Jensen Huang 事件。NVIDIA 四年来对芯片走私中国视而不见。Super Micro 的高管们用吹风机撕掉服务器机架上的标签。法律明令禁止进入中国的芯片直接流向了与 PLA 有关的军事研究单位。Jordan 用银行业合规演进做了精准类比："很长一段时间里，Standard Charter、HSBC、Wells Fargo 基本上不在乎……然后财政部说，不行，你们必须做，否则就罚你们几百亿。"NVIDIA 从未经历过这个合规时刻。

The new export control rule does two things: it requires Commerce Department approval for fabbing advanced chips at TSMC, and it cuts off remote access through Malaysian data centers that currently supply two-fifths of China's AI compute.
