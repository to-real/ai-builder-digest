## No Priors — Tokenization, Agentic Trading, Harmonic: Robinhood's Mission to Expand Ownership with CEO Vlad Tenev

*No Priors*

**Robinhood's agentic trading has 150K accounts and 30M daily tool calls — and Vlad Tenev believes AI discovery must be decentralized or it's dystopian.**

Vlad Tenev, Robinhood CEO and co-founder of math AI startup Harmonic, sees Robinhood as "the ownership company." His conviction comes from growing up in Bulgaria during 2,000% annual inflation, where his grandfather stored wealth in copper cookware because the currency was worthless. That experience forged a belief: broad ownership stabilizes society; concentrated ownership makes it fragile.

Agentic trading is Robinhood's fastest-growing new product — 150,000 accounts opened in months, 30 million tool calls per day, smooth exponential growth. Robinhood Agents (launched at Hood Summit) puts a full trading harness inside your account, like Claude Code but optimized for finance. An app store lets you connect satellite data, build strategies, rebalance portfolios. The north star: put hedge fund capabilities in everyone's pocket.

On tokenization: Robinhood Chain is now the fastest-growing blockchain by developer activity. Stock tokens are DeFi-enabled — they compose like software. Developers are already building stock-token-linked NFTs and meme pairs. For the long tail of countries without functional capital markets, stock tokens do for US equities what stablecoins did for dollars.

On math and AI centralization: Harmonic's Aristotle model won gold at the International Math Olympiad, but Tenev is troubled by AI labs making discoveries in secret data centers. "It's too centralized, right? It's like AI companies with their big data centers and their employees using secret models to sort of do all this work." He believes a random lay person using tools to prove Navier-Stokes would be celebrated; the same result from an OpenAI data center would be rejected. The future he wants: tools in people's hands, discoveries by individuals.

On prediction markets: "Prediction markets are an evolution of news. You just can see what's happening and what's likely to happen in the future through the odds." KPI contracts let you trade specific metrics (EPS, revenue) directly instead of proxying through stock price.

Vlad Tenev 是 Robinhood CEO，也是数学 AI 创业公司 Harmonic 的联合创始人，他把 Robinhood 定位为"所有权公司"。这个信念源于他在保加利亚的成长经历——年通胀 2000%，祖父用铜锅储值因为货币形同废纸。这让他坚信：广泛的所有权稳定社会，集中的所有权让社会脆弱。

Agentic trading 是 Robinhood 增长最快的新产品——数月内 15 万账户开通，每日 3000 万次 tool call，平滑指数增长。Robinhood Agents 把完整的交易 harness 放进你的账户，像 Claude Code 但专为交易优化。应用商店可接入卫星数据、构建策略、再平衡组合。北极星：把对冲基金的能力装进每个人的口袋。

关于 tokenization：Robinhood Chain 已成为开发者活跃度增长最快的区块链。Stock token 全面 DeFi 化——像软件一样可组合。开发者已在构建 stock-token 关联的 NFT 和 meme 对。对那些没有功能资本市场的国家，stock token 对美股的意义等于 stablecoin 对美元的意义。

关于数学与 AI 中心化：Harmonic 的 Aristotle 模型在国际数学奥赛拿金，但 Tenev 对 AI 实验室在秘密数据中心做发现深感不安。"太中心化了，AI 公司用大数据中心和员工用秘密模型做所有工作。"他相信一个普通人用工具证明 Navier-Stokes 会被庆祝，同样的结果来自 OpenAI 数据中心则会被抵制。他想要的未来：工具在人民手中，发现由个人完成。

关于预测市场："预测市场是新闻的进化。你通过赔率就能看到正在发生什么、未来可能发生什么。"KPI 合约让你直接交易具体指标（EPS、收入），而不是通过股价做代理。
