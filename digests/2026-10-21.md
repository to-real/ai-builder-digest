## Aaron Levie

*CEO, Box*

**As models get more capable, domain specialization goes UP, not down — the opposite of the "wrapper death" narrative.**

Levie argues the "bitter lesson" in applied AI is commonly misunderstood. When models were weak, you could only be a wrapper delivering tokens with a custom prompt. But with agents that use tools, run for hours, coordinate swarms, and require domain-specific routing and real change management, specialization actually increases. He predicts autonomous systems will emerge across security, life sciences, healthcare, finance, and manufacturing — job functions that weren't practical to automate before.

Levie 认为 applied AI 领域的"苦涩教训"被普遍误读。模型弱的时候，你只能做 wrapper，把 token 加个 prompt 送回去。但当 agent 能调用工具、长时间运行、协调集群、需要领域专属路由和真正的流程变革时，专业化反而上升。他预测安全、生命科学、医疗、金融、制造等领域将涌现此前无法实现的自主系统。

## AI & I by Every — Why Every Traded Personal Agents for One Company Agent

*AI & I by Every*

**Company agents beat personal agents at work because shared context compounds — but personal agents will thrive where personalization is the whole point.**

Dan Shipper (CEO, Every) and Willie Williams (Head of Platform, Every) trace their full arc: everyone got personal OpenClaw agents, fell in love with customization, then watched them die from maintenance fatigue and security headaches. The decisive insight: "why don't we take all that energy and put it into one agent that the whole company benefits from?" A company agent learns from every employee's usage, accumulates institutional knowledge, and eliminates the chaos of 30 bots running in Slack. They predict a clean split: one company agent at work (where collective context wins), personal agents at home (where customization is the value). On engineering management, Williams finds the job surprisingly unchanged — still a people job — but leverage has increased because the team's leverage has increased. His token usage actually dropped as he moved back into management: "there's no billion token move I can make. It's mostly like, we still need to sit down and have a conversation."

Dan Shipper (CEO, Every) 和 Willie Williams (平台负责人, Every) 回顾了完整历程：全员配备个人 OpenClaw agent，爱上定制化，然后因维护疲劳和安全问题看着它们消亡。关键洞察："为什么不把所有精力投入到一个全公司受益的 agent 上？" 公司 agent 从每个员工的使用中学习，积累组织知识，消除 30 个 bot 在 Slack 里乱跑的混乱。他们预测一个清晰分野：工作时用公司 agent（集体上下文胜出），生活中用个人 agent（定制化是价值所在）。关于工程管理，Williams 发现这份工作出人意料地没变——仍然是人的工作——但杠杆增加了，因为团队的杠杆增加了。他回到管理岗后 token 用量反而暴跌："没有十亿 token 级的操作，大多数时候还是需要坐下来谈。"
