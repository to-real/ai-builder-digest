## Unsupervised Learning — Ep 92: xAI Co-Founder Unpacks the Future of Model Development

*Unsupervised Learning*

**The future of AI lies in "personal AI" that runs locally and is deeply aligned with individual happiness, rather than centralized, one-size-fits-all models.**

AI 的未来在于运行在本地并与个人幸福感深度对齐的 "personal AI"，而不是中心化、一刀切的模型。

Igor Babushkin, co-founder of xAI and now founder of River AI, argues that the current centralized API model is unsustainable and that the future belongs to "personal AI"—agents that live on local hardware, learn continuously from individual users, and optimize for human happiness rather than generic tasks.

xAI 联合创始人、现为 River AI 创始人的 Igor Babushkin 认为，当前的中心化 API 模型是不可持续的，未来属于 "personal AI"——即运行在本地硬件上、持续向个人用户学习并优化人类幸福感而非通用任务的 agent。

He believes we are approaching a bifurcation where super-intelligent AIs serve a few, but everyday people need agents that are deeply personalized, private, and under their direct control.

他预测我们将面临分化：超级智能 AI 服务于少数人，但普通人需要的是深度个性化、私密且受其直接控制的 agent。

Babushkin also predicts that open-source models will soon surpass proprietary ones, squeezing closed-source providers unless they innovate on distribution and utility.

Babushkin 还预测，开源模型很快将超越专有模型，除非闭源提供商在分发和实用性上进行创新，否则将受到挤压。
