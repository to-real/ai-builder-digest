## Training Data — Rich Sutton and Khurram Javed: Why AI Models Stop Learning, and How to Start It Again

*Training Data*

**True intelligence requires continual learning from experience, not just static pre-training on finite data.**

真正的智能需要从经验中持续学习，而不仅仅是静态的预训练。

Rich Sutton argues that the current LLM paradigm is "weird" because it stops learning after deployment, whereas biological intelligence never stops. He and Khurram Javed introduce the "Big World Hypothesis," suggesting the world is infinitely complex and synthetic data is a dead end because it relies on human bottlenecks. Instead, they advocate for algorithms that enable continual deep learning—updating weights in real-time without catastrophic forgetting—allowing agents to form their own abstractions and plan. As Sutton puts it, "I'm not weird. The field is weird. They feel they need to call it continual learning. It's just learning."

Rich Sutton 认为当前的 LLM 范式很“奇怪”，因为它在部署后就停止了学习，而生物智能从未停止。他和 Khurram Javed 提出了“大世界假说”，认为世界无限复杂，合成数据是死胡同，因为它受限于人类的瓶颈。相反，他们主张开发能够实现持续深度学习的算法——在不发生灾难性遗忘的情况下实时更新权重——让 agent 能够形成自己的抽象并进行规划。正如 Sutton 所说：“我不奇怪，是这行奇怪。他们觉得需要称之为‘持续学习’。但这本来就是学习。”
