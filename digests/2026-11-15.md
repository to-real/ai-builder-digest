## Training Data — A Model For Everyone: How Ronak From Trajectory Is Building Products For Continual Learning

*Training Data*

**Continual learning is the next learning paradigm for AI, and the missing layer is a product that turns "what good looks like" into model improvements with one click.**

Ronak, co-founder and CEO of Trajectory (previously at Windsurf where he trained frontier models), argues that today's AI is stuck in "pseudo continual learning" — like using AAA paper maps where every route change requires a full batch recompute. The real goal is Google Maps-style online learning where every trajectory updates the model. His team is building the product layer to close the loop between production traces and model improvement. The core insight: every big tech company (Google, Amazon, Meta) already does continual learning for recommenders and ads, but agents don't have this yet. Trajectory's platform unifies training, eval, and deployment in one place — inspired by Apple combining the internet and the phone into the iPhone. The Harvey case study is striking: they trained a model that went from 4% to 18% accuracy on a legal agent benchmark while being 6x cheaper than frontier models. On the research front, Trajectory is pushing GRPO toward "group size one" — learning from a single rollout instead of needing 16 replays of the same environment — and building meta-graders that let you label just 100 representative traces and propagate reward across 10,000. Ronak distinguishes continual learning from Recursive Self Improvement: "I want to learn from experience, but learn from the experience of actually interacting with humans and then getting better at the tasks that they set out." His vision for product builders is becoming "almost like a gardener" — set the trellises, and the product grows autonomously.

Ronak 是 Trajectory 的联合创始人兼 CEO（此前在 Windsurf 训练前沿模型），他认为今天的 AI 困在"伪持续学习"中，就像用 AAA 纸质地图，每次改路线都要全量重算。真正的目标是 Google Maps 式的在线学习，每条轨迹都更新模型。他的团队正在构建产品层来闭合生产轨迹与模型改进之间的回路。核心洞察：每家大科技公司（Google、Amazon、Meta）已经在推荐和广告上做持续学习，但 agent 还没有。Trajectory 的平台将训练、评估和部署统一在一处，灵感来自 Apple 把互联网和电话合为 iPhone。Harvey 的案例令人瞩目：训练出的模型在法律 agent benchmark 上从 4% 提升到 18% 准确率，同时成本是前沿模型的 1/6。研究方面，Trajectory 正推动 GRPO 走向"group size one"，从单次 rollout 学习而非需要 16 次同环境重放；同时构建 meta-grader，只需标注 100 条代表性轨迹就能将奖励传播到 10,000 条。Ronak 区分了持续学习与递归自我改进："我想从经验中学习，但从与人类交互的经验中学习，然后在他们设定的任务上变得更好。"他对产品 builder 的愿景是变成"几乎像园丁"，搭好支架，产品自主生长。
