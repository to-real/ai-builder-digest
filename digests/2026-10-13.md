## No Priors — Frontier Chips for Frontier AI Labs, with Walter Goodwin, Founder/CEO of Fractile

*No Priors*

**The chip that wins the next decade is the one that solves the memory bandwidth bottleneck, not the FLOPs bottleneck. FLOPs scaled 1M× in 20 years; memory bandwidth only 40×.**

Walter Goodwin founded Fractile in 2022 to build full-stack inference chips for the world's largest models. The core contrarian bet: everyone is optimizing FLOPs, but the real ceiling on inference speed is memory bandwidth. Fractile started with an SRAM-based approach (like Groq) that delivers thousands of tokens/sec but hit a wall on context length scalability. Over the past two years, they pivoted to skunkworks projects with memory vendors to get extremely high bandwidth to DRAM, aiming to unite Groq-class speed with GPU-class capacity. The target: multi-trillion-parameter models running at thousands of tokens/sec with long contexts, which is where fast inference actually moves the dial, not "snappier chatbots." Goodwin calls the snappier chatbot "the faster horses of fast inference" and says the real unlock is making long-running agents radically faster.

Walter Goodwin 于 2022 年创立 Fractile，为全球最大模型打造全栈推理芯片。核心逆向押注：所有人都在优化 FLOPs，但推理速度的真正天花板是 memory bandwidth。Fractile 最初采用 SRAM 方案（类似 Groq），能实现每秒数千 token，但在上下文长度可扩展性上碰壁。过去两年转向与内存厂商合作，争取对 DRAM 的超高带宽访问，目标是统一 Groq 级速度和 GPU 级容量。Goodwin 称更快的聊天机器人是"快速推理中的更快的马"，真正的解锁是让长时间运行的 agent 大幅加速。

The full-stack strategy (150 people spanning architecture, physical design, back-end implementation, and advanced packaging, all in-house) exists because the handoff model (architect → Broadcom → TSMC) creates a latency penalty that kills agility. "If you can just find a way to structurally carve out a three to six months advantage, you will be winning all of those deployments." That 3-6 month structural lead is the chip-space equivalent of a frontier model lead.

全栈策略（150 人横跨架构、物理设计、后端实现和先进封装，全部内部完成）之所以存在，是因为交接模式（架构师 → Broadcom → TSMC）带来的延迟惩罚扼杀了敏捷性。"只要你能结构性领先 3-6 个月，你就能赢下所有部署。"这个结构性领先就是芯片领域的 frontier model 领先。

On market structure: hyperscaler internal chips (TPU, MTIA, Maya, Jalapeno) are architecturally similar because they all flow through the same ASIC houses (mainly Broadcom) and use the same HBM + Tensor Core + TSMC packaging recipe. Their primary purpose is leverage against NVIDIA pricing, not enabling new capabilities. Frontier labs can't go all-in on proprietary silicon because of asymmetric risk: if a rival finds a computational breakthrough that only works on a different chip, you could die in the nine months before you can retool. That structural risk sustains the market for independent frontier chip vendors like Fractile.

市场结构方面：超大规模自研芯片（TPU、MTIA、Maya、Jalapeno）架构相似，因为都走同一批 ASIC 厂商（主要是 Broadcom），用同样的 HBM + Tensor Core + TSMC 封装配方。主要目的是对 NVIDIA 定价的杠杆，而非开启新能力。Frontier lab 不能全押自研芯片，因为不对称风险：如果对手发现一个只在另一款芯片上生效的计算突破，你在重新装备的九个月里可能就死了。这种结构性风险维持了 Fractile 这类独立 frontier 芯片厂商的市场。
