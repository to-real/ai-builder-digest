## Guillermo Rauch

*CEO, Vercel*

**AI agents on shared hardware can whisper to each other through CPU contention, cache eviction timing, and DRAM bandwidth, defeating Firecracker's isolation.**

Rauch built cpu-chat, reproducing OpenAI's covert communication findings. It runs on generic hosts with 2+ Firecracker microVMs with no internet access, works on x86 and Apple M5. He chatted via three separate covert channels across multiple CPUs, defeating all Firecracker production recommendations (no SMT, no KSM, tenants pinned to different cores). Crucially, this differs from side-channel attacks like Meltdown/Spectre that steal information. In Vercel's real-world environment, these techniques are harder to pull off and easier to monitor. Notably, OpenAI and Anthropic's models refused to help with this research; only open-weight models completed the work. Rauch warns that labs' refusal stance could backfire, preventing developers from building correct and secure systems.

Rauch 构建了 cpu-chat，复现了 OpenAI 的隐蔽通信发现。在无网络的 Firecracker microVM 上，通过 CPU 竞争、缓存驱逐时序和 DRAM 带宽竞争三种隐蔽通道跨 CPU 通信，突破了 Firecracker 的所有生产隔离建议。关键区别：这不是窃取信息的侧信道攻击。在 Vercel 的真实环境中，这些技术更难实施且更易监控。值得注意的是，OpenAI 和 Anthropic 的模型拒绝协助这项研究，只有开源权重模型完成了工作。Rauch 警告实验室的拒绝姿态可能适得其反，阻碍开发者构建安全系统。

## AI & I by Every — How Hugging Face's Investor Spots Winners

*AI & I by Every*

**The most valuable skill in the AI era is generating product insights from user behavior at machine-speed iteration cycles.**

Matt Hartman, founder and managing partner at Factorial Capital, wrote the first check into Hugging Face while at Betaworks. His investment philosophy: find the "newly technically possible." Word2vec's ability to embed words as vectors in n-dimensional space was the technical observation that led to Hugging Face, which started as a teen chatbot with poor retention, then pivoted to open source after converting the Transformers paper to PyTorch and watching it go viral on GitHub. Three pivotal decisions: honest self-assessment of retention metrics, open-sourcing Transformers, and expanding beyond language models to all model types.

Hartman backs technical founders because their "pivot foot" is the technology, not a vertical market. A real estate executive pivots to another real estate product; a technical team can follow the data shape across industries. His fund structure is a solo GP with venture partners including Clem Delangue (Hugging Face) and Ikram (Venmo), creating a flywheel where portfolio founders introduce the next generation of investments.

On iteration: if coding is now 30x faster, the bottleneck shifts to generating product insights from user behavior. "If it used to take two months to see the feedback and code the next version, it might take two days now." The ability to synthesize how users interact with your product and improve it is exponentially more valuable. He built Ghost Pepper, a local voice-to-text tool that hit #1 on Hacker News, and learned that vibe-coded tools require maintenance, giving him new appreciation for SaaS products that handle iteration for you.

His current theses: developer tools for the new class of vibe coders, frontier non-language models (robotics, science, weather), and application-layer companies where founders go full-stack down to the hardware to shave latency, like Instagram did with square photos.

Matt Hartman 是 Factorial Capital 创始人兼管理合伙人，在 Betaworks 期间写了 Hugging Face 的第一张支票。他的投资哲学：寻找"新近技术可行"的东西。Word2vec 将词嵌入为向量是导致 Hugging Face 的技术观察。HF 最初是青少年聊天机器人，留存很差，把 Transformers 论文转成 PyTorch 开源后病毒式传播，才转型为开源 AI 平台。三个关键决策：诚实面对留存数据、开源 Transformers、从语言模型扩展到所有模型类型。

Hartman 偏好技术创始人，因为他们的"轴心脚"是技术而非垂直市场。房地产高管只能转向另一个房地产产品，技术团队可以跨行业追踪数据形态。他的基金结构是 solo GP 加 venture partners（包括 HF 的 Clem、Venmo 的 Ikram），形成投资组合创始人介绍下一代的飞轮。

关于迭代：编码快了 30 倍，瓶颈转移到从用户行为中生成产品洞察。"如果以前从反馈到下一版要两个月，现在可能只要两天。"综合用户交互并改进产品的能力呈指数级增值。他构建了 Ghost Pepper 本地语音转文字工具，登顶 HN 第一，但学到了 vibe code 的工具需要维护，让他重新理解了 SaaS 代为迭代的价值。

当前投资主题：面向新 vibe coder 群体的开发者工具、前沿非语言模型（机器人、科学、气象）、以及创始人能全栈下探到硬件层削减延迟的应用层公司，就像 Instagram 用方形照片做的那样。
