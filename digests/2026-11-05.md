## Unsupervised Learning — Ep 96: Nebius CTO on Racing the Hyperscalers Before AWS Wakes Up

*Unsupervised Learning*

**The real moat in AI cloud is software, not GPU access, and the single bottleneck across the entire buildout is execution ability.**

Nebius CTO Neela (formerly of Yandex) lays out why hyperscalers haven't dominated AI cloud: chip shortages force Microsoft and Google to choose between internal consumption and external offering; AI cloud is still too small a revenue slice to justify re-architecting infrastructure built for old workloads; and the required networking and storage overhaul is massive. Nebius, at ~1% of AWS revenue, bets this market explodes 10-100x in three years and that an independent player serving mid-tier companies has a durable niche. The company is software-first: 200 software engineers vs. 40-50 on the hardware side. On inference optimization, Neela is blunt: "as an industry, we didn't optimize shit." 95% of customers run modified, fine-tuned models, not vanilla open source, and for real enterprise adoption, specialized agents in scoped domains will care more about predictability and time-to-outcome than model size. Disaggregated inference (prefill/decode split) is talked about everywhere but done properly nowhere. The most contrarian insight: experienced project managers who can navigate GPU shortages, transformer lead times, and facility constraints simultaneously are the highest-demand role in AI infrastructure right now. As Neela puts it: "your ability to execute around all these problems and concerns... becomes a moat."

Nebius CTO Neela（前 Yandex）解释了超大规模云厂商为何未能主导 AI 云：芯片短缺迫使 Microsoft 和 Google 在内部消耗与外部供给之间做选择；AI 云收入占比太小，不值得为旧工作负载重构基础设施；所需的网络和存储改造规模巨大。Nebius 收入约为 AWS 的 1%，押注该市场三年内爆发 10-100 倍，独立服务商服务中型公司有持久利基。公司以软件为先：200 名软件工程师对 40-50 名硬件人员。关于推理优化，Neela 直言："整个行业什么都没优化好。"95% 的客户运行修改过的 fine-tune 模型而非原生开源模型，真正的企业采用中，限定领域的专业化 agent 更关心结果可预测性和达成时间而非模型大小。Disaggregated inference（prefill/decode 分离）人人都在谈，没人真正做好。最反直觉的洞察：能同时应对 GPU 短缺、变压器交货期和设施约束的经验丰富的项目经理，是当前 AI 基础设施最紧缺的角色。正如 Neela 所说："你在所有这些问题和顾虑中执行的能力……变成了护城河。"
