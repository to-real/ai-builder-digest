## Guillermo Rauch

*CEO, Vercel*

**The Rust-ification calculus has flipped: "best for humans" is no longer "best for business."**

Rauch recounts Vercel's migration of Turborepo from Go to Rust — the ROI was controversial internally because Go is fast, well-designed, and easy to iterate on, while the human migration costs were substantial. But the arrival of AI agents changes the equation: code is increasingly written or modified by agents, so human ergonomics matter less. Rauch also suspects Rust isn't the endgame, since it was designed before the "supersonic tsunami of agents."

Rauch 回顾了 Vercel 将 Turborepo 从 Go 迁移到 Rust 的经历——内部 ROI 争议很大，因为 Go 本身就快、设计优雅、迭代容易，而人力迁移成本很高。但 AI agent 的到来改变了等式：代码越来越多地由 agent 编写或修改，人类工效学的权重下降。Rauch 同时认为 Rust 也不是终局，因为它诞生于 agent "超音速海啸"之前。

→ [原文](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2106863842450133114)

**READMEs are for humans; documentation internals are for agents — and the distinction matters.**

Rauch's rule of thumb: anything for human consumption (blogs, tweets, READMEs) should be written by hand, because it's the storytelling moment to connect with people. Internal documentation can be "AI English" because it's for agents. It's not about em-dashes — it's about audience.

Rauch 的经验法则：面向人类消费的内容（博客、推文、README）应该手写，因为那是与人建立连接的叙事时刻。内部文档可以是"AI English"，因为读者是 agent。关键不在破折号，而在受众。

## No Priors — Re-Founding Incumbents for the AI Era with Sequence Holdings Co-Founder and CEO Michael Lee

*No Priors*

**The biggest alpha in AI isn't starting from zero — it's acquiring incumbents with structural moats and refounding them with frontier engineering.**

Michael Lee spent years covering AI at Lone Pine (starting in 2017, when AlphaGo and the transformer paper landed). His conclusion: AI's economic impact would be deeply uneven. Startups win in categories like coding, but in industries where brand, scale, network effects, and regulation create moats, the incumbent holds all the advantages. Sequence Holdings buys those incumbents and transforms them.

Lee argues three alternatives all fail. Self-transformation can't recruit frontier engineers because the "celebrated persona" of a typical company is the investor, not the engineer. Service providers have an incentives problem: "Services companies optimize for getting in your wallet, staying in your wallet, growing the share of your wallet. It is a path towards incrementalism." Software vendors sell to today's workflows, not the reorganized ones AI enables. Only ownership aligns incentives, lets you celebrate the engineer, and supports long-horizon transformation.

BankSouth proved the thesis in six months: consumer underwriting time dropped 94%, loan processing fell from 30 to 11 days, and Q2 loan volumes doubled with a smaller underwriting team. Regulated industries turned out to be a feature — well-defined rules and clean data are ideal for agents. Their Atlas platform (data ontology, agent builder, orchestration engine Lattice, application builder) captures the 80% of business logic that's homogenous across industries.

Sequence just announced the largest AI take-private to date: insurance brokerage Baldwin with the Dell family office at $7.7B. They target one deal per year. As Lee puts it: "Every company on the planet has a celebrated persona. In a world where you believe that alpha comes from engineering and AI, you need to create a culture whereby the celebrated persona is the engineer."

Michael Lee 从 2017 年起在 Lone Pine 覆盖 AI（AlphaGo 和 transformer 论文问世之时）。他的结论：AI 对经济的影响极不均匀。创业公司在编码等领域会赢，但在品牌、规模、网络效应和监管构筑护城河的行业里，在位企业拥有全部优势。Sequence Holdings 收购这些在位企业并改造它们。

Lee 论证三种替代方案都行不通。自我转型招不到前沿工程师，因为典型企业的"核心人格"是投资人而非工程师。服务商有激励问题："服务公司优化的是进入你的钱包、留在你的钱包、扩大钱包份额。这是走向渐进主义的路径。"软件供应商卖的是今天的工作流，不是 AI 使能的重构后的工作流。只有所有权能对齐激励、让工程师成为核心、支撑长期转型。

BankSouth 在六个月内验证了论点：消费信贷审批时间下降 94%，贷款处理从 30 天降到 11 天，Q2 贷款量翻倍而审批团队更小。受监管行业反而是优势——规则明确、数据干净，正是 agent 的理想环境。他们的 Atlas 平台（数据本体、agent 构建器、编排引擎 Lattice、应用构建器）捕获了跨行业 80% 的同构业务逻辑。

Sequence 刚宣布迄今最大的 AI 私有化交易：与 Dell 家族办公室以 77 亿美元收购保险经纪公司 Baldwin。他们每年只做一个项目。正如 Lee 所说："地球上每家公司都有一个核心人格。在一个你认为 alpha 来自工程和 AI 的世界里，你需要创造一种工程师是核心人格的文化。"
